### 🧩 Structures & Nommage canoniques
- Pages: `index.html`, `about.html`, `best.html`.
- Dossiers: `assets/` (images, fonts), `css/` (styles), `js/` si besoin.
- Entités génériques: [[Page]], [[Component]] (header, nav, footer, card), [[Module]] CSS (layout, utilities), [[Config]].
- Données génériques: id, name, status, created_at, updated_at.
- États: draft | active | archived | error.
- Fichiers génériques: `css/style.css`, `assets/logo.svg`, `assets/img_A.jpg`.
