Terror Angel Ultimate Skill Correction - The Ultimate Skill is "Contrôle de l'Enfer" (Control of Hell), not "Perforation". It attacks targets within 7 m, hits them 5 times, deals 750% total damage, and makes the character invincible during use. The passive skill "Esprit sacré" increases Contrôle de l'Enfer damage by 20%, and "Pouvoir" debuffs enemies hit by Contrôle de l'Enfer, reducing their Attack Power by 15% for 3 seconds.
