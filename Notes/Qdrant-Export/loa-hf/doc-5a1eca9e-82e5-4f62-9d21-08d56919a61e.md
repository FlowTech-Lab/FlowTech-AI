League of Angels Heaven Fury - Enhancement System Breakdown: Equipment enhancement provides stat increases through three systems: Amélioration (Improvement) adds base stat increases, Perfectionnement (Perfection) adds refined stat increases, Augmentation adds additional stat increases. For Arc des forteresses +22: Attack gets +9798 from Improvement, +13567 from Perfection, +12910 from Augmentation (total +123312). Penetration gets +4898 from Improvement, +6783 from Perfection, +12912 from Augmentation (total +61656). Enhancement level +22 indicates high-level equipment enhancement. Each enhancement level increases these three stat components.
