League of Angels Heaven Fury - War Seal System: War Seal system provides additional combat attributes. Character Lilith has multiple war seals: Frappe fatale (Fatal Strike) Level 18 - Attack +7326, Critical +1826, Armor HP +3.96%. Position en équilibre (Balanced Position) Level 2 - Attack +2797, Defense +1403, Armor Defense +3.11%, Accessory Attack +2.69%. Frappe puissante (Powerful Strike) Level 5 - Attack +2250, HP +45000, Armor Defense +3.33%. Flèche perforante (Piercing Arrow) Level 9 - Attack +4329, Penetration +2171, Armor HP +2.34%. War seals provide flat stat bonuses and percentage-based bonuses. Different war seal types focus on different stat priorities. War seal levels determine bonus values. War seals contribute to total BR and character power.
