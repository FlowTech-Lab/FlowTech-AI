League of Angels Heaven Fury - Stat Priorities by Class: DRAGOON priority stats: Defense (highest), HP/Health Points (high), Physical Attack (medium), Magic Defense (medium), Agility (low). MAGE priority stats: Magic Attack (highest), Intelligence (high), Magic Defense (medium), HP (medium), Physical Defense (low). ARCHER priority stats: Physical Attack (highest), Agility (high), Critical Hit Rate (high), HP (medium), Defense (medium). Combat Power (BR) calculation considers all stats but weighted by class specialization. Equipment optimization must align with these stat priorities for maximum effectiveness.
