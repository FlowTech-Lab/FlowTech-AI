League of Angels Heaven Fury - Ultimate Attributes System: Ultimate attributes provide percentage-based bonuses that scale with base stats. Arc des forteresses +22 provides: Attack bonus +4.00% (multiplies total attack), Penetration bonus +4.00% (multiplies total penetration), Speed control capacity +4.00% (affects combat speed), Precision bonus +4.00% (increases hit accuracy), Critical bonus +4.00% (increases critical hit rate), Lethal critical +4.00% (increases lethal critical hit chance). Percentage bonuses become more valuable as base stats increase. Ultimate attributes are separate from base stat increases and provide multiplicative scaling.
