League of Angels Heaven Fury - Build Optimization Strategies: PvP Builds - Focus on burst damage, critical hit rate, and survivability. Dragoon PvP: High defense and HP, crowd control resistance. Mage PvP: High magic attack, area damage, mobility. Archer PvP: High physical attack, critical hit, range advantage. PvE Builds - Focus on sustained damage and resource efficiency. Dragoon PvE: Balanced defense and attack for solo content. Mage PvE: Maximum magic attack for boss farming. Archer PvE: High single-target damage for elite mobs. Balanced Builds - Mix of PvP and PvE stats for versatility. Equipment choices should reflect build goals and complement class strengths.
