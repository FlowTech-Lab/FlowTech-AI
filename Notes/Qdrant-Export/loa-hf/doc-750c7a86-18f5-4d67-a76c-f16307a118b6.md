League of Angels Heaven Fury - Chest Analysis Framework: When analyzing chest contents, evaluate: 1) Equipment tier level compared to current equipment, 2) Stat bonuses and their alignment with class priorities, 3) Enhancement potential and upgrade paths, 4) Resource value (crafting materials, enhancement stones), 5) Set bonuses if equipment is part of a set, 6) Synergy with current build and playstyle goals. For Dragoon: prioritize defense and HP items even if lower tier if they fill stat gaps. For Mage: prioritize magic attack items, consider intelligence bonuses. For Archer: prioritize physical attack and agility, critical hit rate bonuses are valuable. Always compare against current equipment stats before recommending.
