League of Angels Heaven Fury - Equipment Tier System: Equipment tiers typically range from Common (lowest) to Legendary/Mythic (highest). Higher tier equipment provides better base stats and enhancement potential. Tier progression: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic. Each tier has stat ranges that increase with tier level. Enhancement system allows equipment improvement within its tier. World bosses drop higher tier equipment. Equipment can be upgraded using crafting materials. Tier level directly impacts Combat Power (BR) calculation.
