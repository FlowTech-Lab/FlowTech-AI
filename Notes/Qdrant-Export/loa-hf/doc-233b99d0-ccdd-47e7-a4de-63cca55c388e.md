Rahab Angel Passive Skills - Christmas Celebration Event

Rahab Angel has 4 permanent personal passive skills:

1. Providence:
- Type: Personal Passive
- Cooldown: Permanent
- Cost: No cost
- Effect: Permanently increases main character HP by 2500 and Attack by 125
- Analysis: Permanent base stat boost, improves survivability and base damage, always active

2. Esprit sacré (Sacred Spirit):
- Type: Personal Passive
- Cooldown: Permanent
- Cost: No cost
- Effect: Increases Récolte fatale skill damage by 20%
- Analysis: Direct synergy with Ultimate Skill, significant damage boost, multiplies main skill effectiveness

3. Pouvoir (Power):
- Type: Personal Passive
- Cooldown: Permanent
- Cost: No cost
- Effect: Increases Récolte fatale skill usage damage by 15%
- Analysis: Additional synergy with Ultimate Skill, cumulative bonus with Sacred Spirit (total +35% on Fatal Harvest), maximizes main skill effectiveness

4. Punition céleste (Celestial Punishment):
- Type: Personal Passive
- Cooldown: Permanent
- Cost: No cost
- Effect: Significant probability to increase damage dealt to target during an attack by 15% for 3 seconds
- Analysis: Proc-based damage bonus on attacks, 3-second damage window, complements other passive skills

Skill Synergies:
- Sacred Spirit (+20%) + Power (+15%) = +35% damage on Fatal Harvest
- Fatal Harvest goes from 600% to 810% total damage with passives
- Providence provides permanent base stat boost
- Celestial Punishment provides proc damage bonus on all attacks
- Sacred Spirit + Power focus on Ultimate Skill
