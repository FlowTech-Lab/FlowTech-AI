Rahab Angel Ultimate Skill - Récolte fatale (Fatal Harvest)

Skill Type: Ultimate Skill
Skill Name: Récolte fatale (Fatal Harvest)

Skill Description:
- Attacks targets within 7 m
- Hits them 10 times
- Deals 600% total damage
- Character is invincible during use

Skill Analysis:
- Range: 7 meters (medium range)
- Multi-hit: 10 strikes for total of 600% damage
- Invincibility: Complete protection during skill execution
- Usage: Ideal for close combat and dangerous situations

This ultimate skill provides excellent damage output with built-in protection, making it very valuable in PvP and difficult PvE encounters.
