League of Angels Heaven Fury - Wind Attack Gems: Wind attack gems can be socketed into equipment to provide additional stat bonuses. Pendentif des forteresses +21 has 5 wind attack gems providing: +750, +1500, +400, +750, +400 wind attack bonus (total +3800). Wind attack gems provide flat stat increases that add to base equipment stats. Different gem types provide different stat bonuses (fire attack, wind attack, ice attack, lightning attack, etc.). Gem bonuses stack additively. Higher tier gems provide larger bonuses. Wind attack gems are different from fire attack gems and provide wind element bonuses.
