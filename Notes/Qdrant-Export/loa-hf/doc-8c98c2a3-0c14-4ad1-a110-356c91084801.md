League of Angels Heaven Fury - World Boss Rewards: World bosses reset every 24 hours. Rewards include: exceptional equipment (higher tier drops), crafting materials for equipment enhancement, enhancement stones, rare consumables, experience and gold. Boss difficulty scales with player level. Higher damage contribution increases reward quality. Guild coordination improves boss kill efficiency. Boss rewards are primary source of high-tier equipment. Chest rewards from world bosses should be prioritized for equipment upgrades. Boss-specific drops may have unique stat combinations.
