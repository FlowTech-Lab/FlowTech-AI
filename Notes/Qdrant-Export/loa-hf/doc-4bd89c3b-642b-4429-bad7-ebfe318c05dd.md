League of Angels Heaven Fury - Game Overview: MMORPG action 3D developed by CHENGDU DREAMCALLER TECHNOLOGY CO., LTD, published by YOUZU SINGAPORE PTE LTD. Released September 3, 2020 on Steam and GTarcade. Setting: Fantasy world inspired by Western mythology where gods have succumbed to corruption. Players are the chosen ones tasked with gathering legendary angels to restore celestial order. Three playable classes: Dragoon (powerful warrior, close combat), Mage (elemental master, ranged damage), Archer (deadly marksman, long-range precision). Game features: Unique skills per hero and angel, manual or automatic skill combination, real-time PvP and PvE, guild battles, cross-server challenges, world bosses reset every 24 hours, transformation into Light Angel or Dark Angel for powerful abilities. Game modes: Real-time combat, PvP battles in Fiend's Treasure Trove, world boss raids, guild and alliance battles. Progression: Equipment enhancement, magical tools, weapons, skills development, mounts and spirits system. Reception: 58% rating on Steam based on 760 reviews, average 23 players online in October 2025.
