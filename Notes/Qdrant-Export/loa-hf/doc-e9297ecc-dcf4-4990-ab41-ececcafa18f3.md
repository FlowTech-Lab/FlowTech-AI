League of Angels Heaven Fury - Gem System Fire Attack Gems: Fire attack gems can be socketed into equipment to provide additional stat bonuses. Arc des forteresses +22 has 5 fire attack gems providing: +1500, +750, +400, +400, +400 fire attack bonus (total +3450). Gems provide flat stat increases that add to base equipment stats. Different gem types provide different stat bonuses (fire attack, ice attack, lightning attack, etc.). Gem bonuses stack additively. Higher tier gems provide larger bonuses. Gems can be upgraded to increase their bonus values.
