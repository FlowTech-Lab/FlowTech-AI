League of Angels Heaven Fury - Character Classes: DRAGOON - Powerful warrior inheriting dragon strength, specialized in close combat, melee attacks, tank role. MAGE - Master of elements, manipulates nature for massive ranged damage, spellcaster, area of effect abilities. ARCHER - Elite marksman using magic-imbued bows, effective for precise long-range attacks, high single-target damage. Each class has unique exclusive skills that can be combined manually or triggered automatically. Classes can transform into Light Angel or Dark Angel forms for enhanced abilities.
