League of Angels Heaven Fury - PvP Rewards and Fiend's Treasure Trove: Fiend's Treasure Trove is PvP mode where players compete for rewards. Rewards scale with performance and ranking. PvP chests contain: PvP-specific equipment with PvP stat bonuses, honor points, special currency, enhancement materials. PvP equipment often has different stat priorities than PvE equipment. PvP builds benefit from equipment with crowd control resistance, burst damage bonuses, and survivability stats. When analyzing PvP chest rewards, prioritize items that enhance PvP effectiveness over raw stat numbers.
