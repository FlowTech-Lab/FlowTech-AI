Kill Command on cooldown.


Kill Shot whenever it’s available (35% HP and under).


Serpent Sting to keep DoT rolling.


Arcane Shot when you have excess Focus.


Cobra Shot to regenerate Focus (or Steady Shot before level 81).


Multi-Shot if fighting multiple mobs (level 24+).


Focus Fire when your pet reaches 5 stacks of Frenzy (level 32+).


Simple rule: Keep your pet attacking, use Kill Command, and don’t cap Focus.
Where to Level from 1 to 90
Here’s a fast path with optional breaks:


1-20: Racial starting zones


20-58: Eastern Kingdoms or Kalimdor


58-68: Outland


68-78: Northrend


78-85: Cataclysm zones (Mount Hyjal is a solid start)


85-90: Pandaria (Jade Forest > Valley of the Four Winds)


Remember: Each expansion jump brings better gear and more XP, so don’t overstay your welcome in old zones.
Bonus Leveling Tips from a Hunter Main


Turn on Pet Taunt (Growl) while solo. Turn it off in dungeons.


Use Feign Death if you overpull and things get dicey.
