Here are some of the Minor Glyphs worth considering:
Glyph of Mend Pet – Mend Pet also makes your pet a little bit happier. This is fantastic and drastically cuts down how much you need to feed your pet, making leveling cheaper and simpler. I strongly recommend you get this ASAP.Glyph of Revive Pet – Reduces pushback when casting Revive Pet by 100%, making it a lot easier to revive your pet in a pinch. Glyph of Scare Beast – Reduces pushback when casting Scare Beast, making it a lot easier to scare an animal away from you. Not useful often but it’s something.Glyph of Feign Death – Reduces the cooldown of Feign Death by 5-seconds. We don’t have a lot of great Minor Glyphs so some people might take this to guarantee Feign Death is always ready when they need it most.
