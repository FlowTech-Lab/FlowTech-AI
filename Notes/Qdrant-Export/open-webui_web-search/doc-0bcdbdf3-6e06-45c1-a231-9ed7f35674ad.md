Engineering —
Engineering is still by far the best profession in the game, made even stronger in Mists of Pandaria
with the on-use glove tinker  Synapse Springs. This will be far and away the best option
as a DPS player. You also cannot ignore the utility of  Nitro Boosts, which provide
a ton of movement speed on-demand. Given that Engineering is by far the best DPS increase
and provides excellent utility bonuses, it's a must-have profession.
 Blacksmithing —
