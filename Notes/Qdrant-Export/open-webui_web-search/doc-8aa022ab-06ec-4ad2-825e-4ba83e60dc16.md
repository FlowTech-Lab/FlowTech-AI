You can approach professions while leveling in one of two ways: you can either start working on the skills you want to use at level 90 or you can pick up some professions to help you along the way. It can be more efficient in the long run to level your endgame professions at the same time that you level your character, but most crafting professions will significantly slow down the leveling process if you do this. There are also some that are much easier to level after you get to Pandaria, like Fishing and Cooking, which can easily be leveled from 1 to 600 on the new continent.
As far as professions that specifically make leveling easier, gathering skills are the best choice. Herbalism, Mining, and Archaeology will give you character experience as you gain skill. On top of that, all three primary gathering professions – Herbalism, Mining, and Skinning – each offer useful passive bonuses that can help you in your journey, even if you don’t bother to level them at all.
