Comment by TsaniUsing the "Follow" and "Passive" commands can often be a good way of getting your pet unstuck if it gets stuck on a piece of the environment.If that doesn't help, use dismiss (NOT release) and re-call your pet. Places where pets can get really stuck is doorways (especially with stairs right behind them) and walkways/gangplanks/bridges.---If you are having trouble surviving, try using a Spirit Beast. Use the Spirit Beast's Healing spell to heal yourself when needed, have it on auto for it to use it on itself, heal it with Mend Pet, and use Exhilaration for a big heal for one or both. It may take a little practice, but eventually you can weave those small heals into the small pauzes within your rotation when (nearly) everything else is on cooldown. Since both are HoTs, you can also try using them just before a big hit. When grouped or out in the wilds, feel free to use Spirit Heal to help out a fellow player.
