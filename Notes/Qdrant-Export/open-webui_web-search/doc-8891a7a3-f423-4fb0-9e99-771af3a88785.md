Fig. 14: Porada cat. no. 159, Hahn 35 = IAA 65-32 

Shell (gastropod,) 22x11.5 mm. The tightly packed composition comprises 
two crossed lions, each attacking a horned animal. Terminal: a scorpion 
separated by horizontal line from the empty space above, intended for in¬ 
scription. Porada included the seal within the Akkadian dissertation cata¬ 
logue (pp. 42-43). In her hand on the card: “The seal could be ED II but 
corporeality of figures speaks against that.” Also in her hand at bottom 
right of the card: ED III. 

Fig. 15: Porada cat. no. 160, Hahn 46 = IAA 1965-33 

Lapis lazuli, 16.8x9.7 mm. This even more archaic seal is “so fine one 
could imagine it being worn by a child” (p. 43). The tightly packed group 
comprises two crossed lions attacking bull and horned animal respectively. 
Terminal: scorpion separated by two horizontal lines from the space above 
intended for inscription. Porada’s note on the card: cf. Corpus I: 84.
