MoP Classic Survival Hunter Stat Priority
Reach your Hit and Expertise cap, so around 7.5% for both. Then, Agility is your best stat. As soon as a gear piece gives you more Agility, it’s a good idea to equip it.
After that, it goes: Critical Strike, Mastery and then Haste.
