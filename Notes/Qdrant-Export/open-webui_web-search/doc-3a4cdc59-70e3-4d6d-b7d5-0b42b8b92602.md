Consumables
Several categories of consumables boost the health and hitpoints of your character in Survival Hunter’s Classic PvE battles.
The first kind is Food that includes Sea Mist Rice Noodles, and Seasoned Pomfruit Slices. Another kind of consumables is Feast, which includes Great Banquet of the Wok and Banquet of the Wok. The best kind is Potions that grant a huge agility boost and are ideal for cooldown attacks. They include Virmen’s Bite.
Professions
Professions play a vital role in maximizing your Hunter’s performance. The professions you choose alter your Hunter’s behavior and economy, so choosing a profession is a big task. There are several professions to choose from, but the best ones are listed below:


Engineering: Enables Synapse Springs and Nitro Boosts


Leatherworking: Unlocks Draconic Leg Reinforcements for agility, which is one of the early crafted gears. 


Alchemy: Enables Mixology for increased flask duration and effect
