The Peak of Serenity provides Monks with a lot of conveniences for leveling
 
Talent Builds
Monks are a hybrid class who can specialize in tanking, healing, or melee DPS. All three builds are viable for leveling. You can choose the  Windwalker specialization for quickly dispatching enemies with powerful burst damage. You can be a  Brewmaster tank for fast dungeon queues. Or you can choose  Mistweaver for some impressive AoE and survivability.
