Armor: Leather until level 40, then Mail. By level 50, you’ll get a bonus for wearing all Mail gear.


Best Pets for Leveling
Your pet is half your toolkit, and choosing the right one matters:


Tenacity Pets (Tanky): Best for leveling. Bears and Turtles have defensive cooldowns that make them hard to kill.


Ferocity Pets (DPS): Faster kills, but less survivability. Great once you overgear zones. Cats and Raptors fit here.


Utility Pets (Buffs): Some pets bring valuable buffs like Bloodlust (Core Hound) or healing (Spirit Beasts).


In Pandaria specifically, Spirit Beasts like the Savage family are highly sought after; they’re rare, flashy, and bring self-heals. For pure efficiency, though, stick to a sturdy Tenacity pet until at least 85.
