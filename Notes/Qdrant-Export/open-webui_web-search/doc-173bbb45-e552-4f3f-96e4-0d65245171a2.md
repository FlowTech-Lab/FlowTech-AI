Loot Priority: Always grab ranged weapon upgrades in dungeons. A single bow/gun drop can outclass 10 quest greens.


Professions While Leveling
Professions can feel like a time sink, but Hunters benefit from a couple during the climb:


Skinning + Leatherworking: You’ll be killing beasts constantly, so skinning them for leather is natural. Leatherworking lets you craft your agility gear.


Herbalism + Mining: If gold-making is your goal, double gathering is king. Flight in Pandaria makes farming nodes ridiculously efficient.


Engineering: A luxury choice, but fun for toys and ranged weapon crafts.


Leveling Tips & Tricks for Hunters
Even as one of the easiest leveling classes, a few tricks make the journey smoother:


Manage Pet Aggro: Always keep Growl on while questing solo. Toggle it off in dungeons to avoid stealing threat from tanks.


Use Mend Pet Constantly: It’s cheap and keeps your pet alive so you don’t waste time resummoning.
