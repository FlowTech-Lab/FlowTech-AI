Heroes and animals in contest 

The most popular theme in Akkadian seals is the hero-animal combat sce¬ 
ne. The theme, which includes combinations of men, monsters and wild 
animals originates in Early Dynastic glyptic art and continues down to the 
Kassite period. The change is in style. Overcoming a powerful beast or 
monster must have carried some organizing and governmental significance.
