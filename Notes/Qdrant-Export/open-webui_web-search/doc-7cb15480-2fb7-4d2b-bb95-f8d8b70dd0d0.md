by the “Melissa group,” named after a seal discovered by Porada during her 
excavations at the site of Melissa near Phlamoudhi (Fig. 18). Characterized 
by a style with a “smooth plain surface of the (animal) body and the pattern 
of sharp lines with which details are indicated,” she noted Aegean parallels 
for both these and other elements of style and postures such as the lion with
