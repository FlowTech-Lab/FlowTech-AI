The Cypriote cylinder Fig. 13 provides some guidance for the iconography 
of this group. It shows the enthroned goddess holding an ax with the blade 
pointing outward for action. In front of her the bull-headed demon holds a lion 
by the head and the tail, obviously delivering the beast for judgment to the 
goddess. One may assume that the lion represents a criminal element that is 
about to be punished.
