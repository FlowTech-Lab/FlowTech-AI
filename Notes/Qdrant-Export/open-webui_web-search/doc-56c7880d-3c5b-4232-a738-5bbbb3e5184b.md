Is Beast Mastery Hunter Good?
Beast Mastery is still one of the best leveling specs, allowing you to play an easy to learn specialization with your pets dealing a lot of damage. But, at max level, Beast Mastery falls off pretty hard in the first tier. It gets a lot better in later patches like SoO, but for the first few months you might be lower on the damage meters than the other specs available to Hunter, and other specs in the game.
Best Talents & Glyphs for Beast Mastery Hunter in MoP Classic
