A new type of cylinder became prevalent only in the time of the Neo- 
Babylonian empire. It is the well known representation of a worshipper 
standing before symbols as seen on Fig. 32. Numerous imprints of such 
seals have been found on the tablets of the Neo-Babylonian empire 1 . It is 
this type of seal, therefore, which has been generally regarded as the char¬ 
acteristic representative of Neo-Babylonian glyptic art.
