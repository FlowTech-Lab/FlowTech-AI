New vlan at one of our providers
One of our providers wanted us to move our machines over to a new vlan
for various reasons. We of course wanted to move things on our timeframe
not all at once, so I setup a second bridge on those machines with the
new vlan tagged, then gradually moved vm's to use that bridge and have
new ips. linux-system-roles/network makes this super easy!
https://pagure.io/fedora-infra/ansible/blob/main/f/inventory/host_vars/ibiblio05.fedoraproject.org#_72
Just tells it to make a br1 bridge on the bond0 devce (two physical
network cards) and tag it with vlan 651. So nice.
I have to say I really like linux-system-roles/network.
Everything is configured nicely, no worry about reconfiguring and
suddenly not being able to stay connected, ability to switch
things smoothly. It's great.
Anyhow, all the vm's are now moved over and we will be having a outage
on the 22nd to drop the old vlan and set the new one as default.
Look for an outage ticket on that.
