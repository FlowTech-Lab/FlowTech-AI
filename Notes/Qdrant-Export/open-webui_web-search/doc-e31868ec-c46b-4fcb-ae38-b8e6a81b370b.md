Weapon > Trinkets > Chest > Shoulders in terms of priority. These boost XP gain and save you from constantly replacing gear.
Best Professions for Leveling
Go for gathering professions to earn extra XP and gold as you level.


Herbalism, Mining, or Skinning: All give XP and passive buffs.


Archaeology: Can grant XP and is a fun way to break the monotony.


Crafting professions (like Leatherworking or Engineering) are better saved for endgame. If you're just trying to hit 90 fast, gather and go.
Hunters are also amazing gatherers. Your pet can distract mobs while you gather or let you Feign Death if you’re ambushed.
Hunter Leveling Rotation (BM Focused)
Here’s your go-to rotation from early to endgame:


Keep Aspect of the Hawk up at all times.


Use Hunter’s Mark on every new target.


Kill Command on cooldown.


Kill Shot whenever it’s available (35% HP and under).
