As to the storm god in these scenes, if we identify him with the supreme 
god Teshshup, his function remains to be explained. He merely seems to 
have been an onlooker, perhaps he was meant to be a supporting presence, 
like the great gods in their chariots in the topmost register of the Hasanlu 
bowl (Fig. 4). This is the extent to which we can go in the interpretation of 
these scenes.
