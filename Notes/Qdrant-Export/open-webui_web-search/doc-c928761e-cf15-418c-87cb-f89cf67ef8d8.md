‘There are three pieces of pottery which seem to me to be genuine Mycenaean imports: 

T. No. 29-103-412, Level IX, Room 1337. Wall sherd. On a cream ground sections of three 
undulating lines of dark brown paint. Two of them have a solid white centre line; one has a 
row of white dots. No doubt they represent the arms of an octopus. This piece must have 
come from a large and handsome vase. 

‘2. No. 29-102-919, Level VIII, Room 1303. Three sherds; two join to form the shoulder of a 
stirrup jar. The third is probably from the same vase. The clay is cream colour with 
encircling lines in dark brown. The shape is possibly related to the following piece.
