In the gear planner below the set is "Absolute" BiS and optimized for maximum dps potential. There are a few pieces of gear and gems you can change around based on your own personal situation. The biggest obstacle that most people will run into is obtaining Scaled Greaves of the Marksman from the world boss Doom Lord Kazzak. An easily obtainable alternative is Skulker's Greaves and these do not change the rest of the gearset. The only other important piece of information to take into consideration is your melee weapons. Mooncleaver is the BiS melee weapon if you plan to melee weave. It is advised that you use a cheaper alternative weapon from dungeons/heroics and practice and see if you enjoy melee weaving and can do it properly before spending a ton of gold on leveling Blacksmithing and making Mooncleaver. If you decide that you do not want to melee weave during TBCC your best option is to dual wield.Claw of the Watcher and Blade of the Unrequited with weightstones/sharpening
