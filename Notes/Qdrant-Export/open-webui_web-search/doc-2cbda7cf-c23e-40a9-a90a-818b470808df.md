Drawing by Edith Porada 1938: 194, no. 12. 

Hard black serpentine - Black chert, very fine grained, 27.7x18.1 mm. Two 
horned animals are attacked by lions grasping their necks. Between the 
hind legs of the attacked animals there is a V-shape, which, according to 
Porada, is a schematic reminder of the human tamer of the contestant pairs. 
The addorsed attacked animals looking backwards impart great vigor to this 
cross-shaped centered scene. Terminal: sun god sign. 

Published: Porada 1938: 194.193, no. 12; Boehmer 1965: no. 382, pp. 17, 18, 85; 
Rohn 2011: no. 740.
