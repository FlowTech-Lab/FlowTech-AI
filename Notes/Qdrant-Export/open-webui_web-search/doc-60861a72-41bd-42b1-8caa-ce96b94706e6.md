We see this trait on a fragment of a seal impression from Konar Sandal 
South which preserves the head of a female with a plant emerging from the 
top of her head. (Fig. 22a, b). On one of the seals from Tepe Yahya (Fig. 
8b) a standing, certainly divine, female also has a plant emerging from the 
center of her head and similar elements emerging from the shoulders. 
While at Konar Sandal South both male and female deities are shown 
wearing a single crescent pair of horns, females are never shown wearing 
animal headdresses of any kind. Is it possible that this closer association to 
the essential animal nature suggests a more chthonic or totemic aspect to 
the divine or heroic male figures? Females are associated with grain and 
plants. Even on the Rosen 2 seal the enthroned female holds a heavily 
leafed branch.
