P.0.W. x2 (Early Season, costs 2 Sparks) — This
item is not our long-term BiS, but it is a very strong early Season
option. It is not as good as the 2x  Dawnthread Lining setup in the long
run. The second boss on Mythic difficulty will drop a better Weapon, and you
can get better weapons from the Great Vault. Crafting a Weapon does
have some risk factor to it for this reason.
(BiS, 1 Spark) —  Glyph-Etched Gauntlets with  Dawnthread Lining — Note that if Crafting this right away will mess with your Tier Set acquisition, you may want to go for Bracers first.
(BiS, 1 Spark) —  Glyph-Etched Vambraces with  Dawnthread Lining
Next, chase Item Level where needed, preferring Weapon > Helm/Chest/Legs (though these are likely occupied by tier) > Boots/Belt/Shoulders > Rings/Necks > Cloaks.
At this point, you are mostly done crafting. You can spend future Sparks on off-spec items, alternatively statted crafts for different situations, and so on.
