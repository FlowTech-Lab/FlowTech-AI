Glyph of Animal Bond
One of the mandatory glyphs for raiding! It enhances all healing you and your pet receive while your pet is alive by 10%.
Glyph of Deterrence
This glyph is yet another one of the most potent glyphs for any PvE environment! It is useful for solo play, dungeons, and raids, especially for encounters where you must tank a mob or soak a dangerous mechanic. 
Glyph of Endless Wrath
This glyph is very useful for any encounter where there is a lot of AoE splash damage that can kill your pet quickly! It is also especially good for quickly clearing AoE groups that cleave in solo play, ensuring that your pet remains alive. 
Glyph of Liberation
A viable, yet underwhelming choice when compared to the rest, this glyph grants you a bit of self-healing.
