Beast Mastery Hunter AoE Rotation
Keep  Serpent Sting up if you’re fighting against 2-3 enemies. Use  Multi-Shot to apply  Beast Cleave and follow your single-target priority.
In fights against 5 or more enemies, a Beast Mastery  Hunter uses the following priority list:
Cast  Multi-Shot as much as possible.
Cast  Kill Shot if possible.
Cast  Glaive Toss whenever it’s ready.
Cast  Cobra Shot.
 Explosive Trap is only a DPS increase if it hits have 5 or more enemies for the full duration.
