Interloper's Chain Boots - Boots from The Soul Hunters.
  These simply increase the proc rate of your Cloak and are always BiS. Do not craft Boots, you will want these even from lower difficulties if you can.
 Band of the Shattered Soul - Ring from Dimensius The All Devouring, the final boss. Gives you occasional Cosmic damage reduction. The stats on this ring are nothing special, and we are not likely to use for the Cosmic damage reduction alone.

The stats of the  Reshii Wraps are modified by the Fiber Socket, which can be swapped at any time out of
combat. These Fiber Socket Gems can be acquired at Rare/Blue quality from Hashim on K'aresh, or crafted by Tailoring at Epic/Purple
quality. As Marksmanship, you should generally use
 Precise Fiber /  Pure Precise Fiber to get Crit.
