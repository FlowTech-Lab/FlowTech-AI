Dominique Collon studied art history in Belgium for two years before going to 
London to study archaeology. She wrote her dissertation at Columbia Universi¬ 
ty in New York under Edith Porada, The seal impressions from Tell Atcha- 
na/Alalakh (published in 1975). Besides being editor of Iraq and curator in the 
Department of the Ancient Near East at the British Museum, she was project 
leader of the Catalogue of the Western Asiatic Seals in the British Museum 
from 1980s until 2009. She has taken part in excavations in Iraq, Turkey, and 
Syria.
