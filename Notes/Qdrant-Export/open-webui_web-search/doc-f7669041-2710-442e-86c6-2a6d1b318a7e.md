Level 30: Exhilaration – Instant heal for both you and your pet.


Level 45: Aspect of the Iron Hawk – Reduces damage taken, great for survivability.


Level 60: Dire Beast synergy or Fervor for focus regen.


Level 75: Blink Strikes – Huge boost to pet damage.


Level 90: Glaive Toss – Strong AoE for multiple mob pulls.


For glyphs, focus on Glyph of Animal Bond (increases healing to you and your pet) and Glyph of Mending (buffs Mend Pet).

Gear & Stats Priority for Leveling
Unlike endgame raiding, leveling gear is about efficiency, not min-maxing. Still, knowing which stats matter speeds things up:


Primary Stat: Agility – directly boosts ranged attack power.


Secondary Priority: Crit > Haste > Mastery. Crit smooths your damage, while Haste speeds up shots.


Weapons: Always upgrade your ranged weapon first - your bow, gun, or crossbow is your main damage source. A green-quality weapon upgrade can sometimes feel like doubling your DPS.
