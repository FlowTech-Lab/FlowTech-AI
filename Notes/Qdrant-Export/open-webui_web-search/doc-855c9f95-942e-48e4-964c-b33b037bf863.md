Stat Priorities
To make the most out of Survival Hunter, your stat build should follow this priority list:


Hit & Expertise: Your first and foremost build should be the hit and expertise with each cap at 7.5% which ensures none of your shots miss or get dodged. This is crucial for consistent damage output, especially during raids.


Agility: Being one of the primary stats, your next target should be agility. It increases your attack power and crit chance, making it your best overall stat. Always choose gear with Agility as the main stat if possible.


Critical Strike: After hit and expertise are capped along with agility, Crit becomes your most valuable secondary stat, as it increases the chance that your shots deal double damage, and many of your abilities, including Explosive Shot, benefit immensely from it.
