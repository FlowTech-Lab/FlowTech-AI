A strong leveling path is Borean Tundra → Dragonblight → Zul’Drak → Sholazar Basin. These zones connect well and minimize unnecessary travel.



Beast Mastery Hunters stand out during this stretch since their pets can tank almost any elite, turning what would normally be group quests into easy solo kills.


Levels 80-85: Cataclysm Zones
This bracket is shorter but still important for fast progression.


Mount Hyjal remains the quickest entry point with dense questing.



After Hyjal, either Deepholm or Uldum keeps the pace strong.



While Vashj’ir offers a unique underwater experience, it’s slower unless you specifically enjoy the theme.


Levels 85-90: Pandaria
The grind truly sets in once you reach Pandaria. Sticking to the main questlines is crucial for keeping momentum.


Jade Forest (85-86): Introductory hub with heavy story beats and plenty of linear quests.
