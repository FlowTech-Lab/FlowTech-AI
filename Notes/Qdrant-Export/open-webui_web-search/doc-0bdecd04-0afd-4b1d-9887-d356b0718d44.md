Good examples of compartmental seals with geometric patterns from East Iran, comparable 
to those of the sealings found in Nubia are reproduced by C. C. Lamberg-Karlovsky and M. 
Tosi, “Shahr-i Sokhta and Tepe Yahya: Tracks on the Earliest History of the Iranian Plat¬ 
eau”, East and West , New Series 23 [1973], Figs. 32-49 from periods II and III at Shahr-i 
Sokhta; Fig. 99:L, a button-back steatite stamp seal from Tepe Yahya, Level IYB. 

From Turkmenistan and Afghanistan, ancient Bactria, seals of this type were published by 
Y. M. Masson and V. I. Sarianidi, Central Asia [Southampton 1972], p. 143, middle left; also 
V. I. Sarianidi, Drevnie zemledel’tsi Afganistana [Moscow 1977], pi. 1:1, 6 (the latter seal, of 
copper, seems to have been identical with the second impression from Nubia), 9; pi. Ill: 1- 
6 (passim); p. 85, Fig. 44:14-19; p. 87, Fig. 45, passim; p. 94, Fig. 48:2,3,4,7; p. 95, Fig. 49; 
p. 50, Fig. 50:3.
