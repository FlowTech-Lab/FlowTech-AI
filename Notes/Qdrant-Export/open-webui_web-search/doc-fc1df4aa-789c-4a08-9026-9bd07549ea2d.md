As to the scenes as a whole, only few can be reconstructed. Figs. 2 and 
3 show suppliant goddess and worshipper before an enthroned king or a 
god, a scheme derived from the Third Dynasty of Ur; there, however, the 
goddess usually precedes the worshipper as was probably the case in Fig. 5. 
It might be added that the somewhat unusual scheme of Fig. 2, in which a 
nude attendant stands behind the throne, is paralleled by an imprint on an 
Old Assyrian tablet found in level III at Kanis. 24
