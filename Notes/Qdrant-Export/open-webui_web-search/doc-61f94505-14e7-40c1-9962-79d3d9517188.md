Another row of figures (Fig. 23), again in the British Museum, lacks the 
characteristic goddess with horned miter. Instead it has a female figure that is 
the only one in the row holding a weapon in the same hand with which she 
holds up an animal by its hind leg. In this row the animals being raised are a 
goat, a dog and a lion. Should we think of a hunt in which the dog helps over¬ 
power the lion, which aims to kill the goat? Or are all these animals considered 
noxious?
