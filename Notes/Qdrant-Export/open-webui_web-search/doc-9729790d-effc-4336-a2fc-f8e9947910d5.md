Another Middle Assyrian element is the cock. It is seen on one of the rare 
objects preserved from this period, an engraved ivory pyxis (ill. 8-9), which 
shows little cocks perched on branches in the crowns of trees. Such cocks 
must have been Indian fowl, much smaller than modern chickens, which 
could fly high into trees. Chickens were imported also into Egypt in the 
middle of the second millennium, 25 and it is assumed that they came from 
Mesopotamia, although they seem to have become common there only in 
the Neo-Babylonian period during the sixth century B.C., probably having
