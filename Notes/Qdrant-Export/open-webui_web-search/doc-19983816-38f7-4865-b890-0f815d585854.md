While all of these seals are related iconographically, they are carved in a 
variety of distinct styles. The composition of the Foroughi white stone seal 
is dense, made up of small figures formed through the graver and the drill 
arranged in informal registers. The same general compositional features are 
shared in the Rosen 1 seal, the Bailey seal, and the two register seal from 
Gonur.
