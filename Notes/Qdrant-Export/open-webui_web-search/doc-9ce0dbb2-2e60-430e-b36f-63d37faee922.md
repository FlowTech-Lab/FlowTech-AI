Questing Routes & Leveling Zones
Hunters are one of the most efficient solo-levelers in MoP Classic, thanks to their reliable pets and strong ranged toolkit. While you can practically clear any questline alone, following the right zones makes the journey much smoother. Here’s the recommended path from 1 to 90:
Levels 1-60: Classic Azeroth
The revamped Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor zones provide a fast and structured route.


Horde: It begins with Durotar, pushing through the Barrens, and then into Thousand Needles.



Alliance: Begin in Elwynn Forest, continue through Westfall, and then into Redridge Mountains.


Hunters rarely need dungeons here since pet-tanking allows you to breeze through most quests without downtime.
Levels 60-80: Outland or Northrend
Once you leave Azeroth behind, you’ll have two choices: Outland or Northrend, out of which Northrend usually offers a smoother quest flow and less downtime.
