Level 30: Blinding Shot - Fires a projectile that stuns the enemies within 5 yards of the landing arrow for 10s. Provides great AoE impact in battle.


Level 45: Aspect of the Iron Hawk - Increases ranged attack power by 25%, and reduces all damage taken by 10%.


Level 60: Fervor - Restores Focus to you and your pet, additionally over time for 10s.


Level 75: A Murder of Crows - Summons a flock of crows to attack the target over the next 30s. If used on a target below 20% health, the cooldown is reduced to 60s.


Level 90: Glaive Toss - Hurls two glaives at the target, each dealing damage to each enemy struck and reducing movement speed by 30% for 3s. The primary target takes 4 times as much damage from each strike.
