Stick with Tenacity and go for pet families that bring helpful abilities like Shell Shield or Blood of the Rhino.
Stat Priority for Leveling
No matter your spec, your main stat is Agility. Beyond that:


BM Priority: Agility > Haste > Mastery > Crit


MM Priority: Agility > Crit > Haste > Mastery


Survival Priority: Agility > Crit > Haste > Mastery


Always pick gear with Agility first. Weapon upgrades are your biggest damage spikes, so keep an eye out.
Best Gear & Heirlooms for Leveling
If you have access to heirlooms, grab these:


Weapon: Charmed Ancient Bone Bow


Helm: Tarnished Raging Berserker’s Helm


Shoulder: Champion Herod's Shoulder


Chest: Champion’s Deathdealer Breastplate


Legs: Tarnished Leggings of Destruction


Trinkets: Swift Hand of Justice x2


Ring: Dread Pirate Ring (if you have it)


Back: Inherited Cape of the Black Baron
