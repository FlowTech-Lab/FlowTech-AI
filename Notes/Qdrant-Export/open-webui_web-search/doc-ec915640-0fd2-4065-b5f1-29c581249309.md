Is Survival Hunter Good?
Yes, Survival Hunter is very good. Hunters have two very good specializations in Beast Mastery and Survival. Survival will have a better DPS early on but gets out scaled by Beast Mastery. Luckily, these two have a pretty similar play style and you can swap from one to the other easily. With good DPS options, a simple play style, and interesting utility in Hunter’s Mark, Survival Hunters are a very safe pick in Mists of Pandaria Classic.
