Black serpentine - Greenish chert, 25x17.5 mm. Centered scene: crossed 
lions attacking horned animals looking backwards. Terminal: crescent 
moon. In her dissertation Porada commented on the ornamental effect of 
this highly stylized depiction, resulting in almost linear animals (p. 43). 

Published: Porada 1938: 195.193, no. 13; Boehmer 1965, 16, 17, no. 347.
