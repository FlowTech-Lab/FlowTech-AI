1. are not beaked like those of Minoan and Mycenaean representations. R.S. Merrillees

Merrillees, The Cypriote Bronze Age Pottery Found in Egypt (Studies in Mediterranean 
Archaeology, Yol. XVIII, 1968) pp. 163, and 181, Pis, XV: 1 and XXIV: 2. He also 
pointed out that the handle descends from the rim to the shoulder, instead of from the 
neck which would correspond to the actual shape. Merrillees also mentioned that this 
seems to have been a matter of conventional representation of such vessels because the 
Red Lustrous spindle bottles are shown in the same manner (Merrillees, op. cit ., p. 173).
