Cast  Fervor if you have less than 50 Focus.
Cast  Explosive Shot whenever it’s ready.
Cast  Kill Shot when it’s available.
Cast  A Murder of Crows whenever it’s ready.
Cast  Black Arrow whenever it’s ready.
Apply  Serpent Sting if it’s off. It will be refreshed by  Cobra Shot.
Cast  Barrage /  Glaive Toss whenever it’s ready.
Cast  Arcane Shot if you have 70 or more Focus and nothing else to spend Focus on.
Cast  Cobra Shot.
Always be in  Aspect of the Hawk /  Aspect of the Iron Hawk. You don’t need to cast  Hunter's Mark as it will be applied by  Explosive Shot.
Optimize your DPS during your opener by casting  Explosive Shot >  Black Arrow >  Rapid Fire together with all your DPS cooldowns into  Fervor >  A Murder of Crows. Then continue with the rotation listed above. 
In fights where you can’t use  Rapid Fire twice, it is recommended to save  Rapid Fire for the execute phase.
