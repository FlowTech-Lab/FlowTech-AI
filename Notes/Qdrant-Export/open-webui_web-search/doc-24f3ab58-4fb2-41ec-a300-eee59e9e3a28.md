Hunters are able to equip Bows, Crossbows, and Guns as well as Fist Weapons, Polearms, Staves, and both One-Handed and Two-Handed Axes and Swords. The Ranged weapon slot has been removed in Mists of Pandaria, with Ranged weapons instead being used in the Main Hand slot. Hunters will not be able to use many of their abilities if they do not have a Ranged weapon equipped, so they will always want to use a Bow, Crossbow, or Gun. Ranged weapons no longer require ammunition as they did in the vanilla days. The minimum range for Ranged weapons has also been removed, allowing you to fire them even at point blank. Weapon upgrades will often be the biggest damage increase while leveling. If you’re using an Heirloom weapon, it will upgrade automatically each time you level.
