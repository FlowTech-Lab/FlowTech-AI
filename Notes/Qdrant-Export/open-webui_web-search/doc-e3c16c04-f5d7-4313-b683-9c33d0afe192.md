Welcome to Wowhead's Guide for Elemental Rune & Protocol Inferno Dungeons, an iteration of the Titan Rune Dungeon feature added in Wrath of the Lich King Classic. This Guide will explain how to activate the Mysterious Elemental Device in Heroic Dungeons to enable Protocol Inferno, what changes in each Dungeon, as well as Rewards and Achievements for Protocol Inferno Dungeons.Protocol Inferno Dungeon OverviewProtocol Inferno Dungeons are a new iteration of the Titan Rune Dungeon added in Wrath of the Lich King Classic. This feature exists as a catch-up mechanic, allowing players to gear up for current content without having to go through previous raid tiers. Titan Rune Dungeons were described as and commonly referred to as "Heroic Plus" by the community, as the enemies and bosses inside had increased damage, increased Health, and brand new mechanics.
