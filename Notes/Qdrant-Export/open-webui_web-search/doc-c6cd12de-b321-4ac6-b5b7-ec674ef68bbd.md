Best Gear & Stats for Survival Hunter in MoP Classic
Best in Slot (BiS Gear) aren’t as big in Mists of Pandaria Classic. Reforging is the new hype, so you can tailor your gear better than before.
The gear you should aim for are: Trinkets, Tier Sets and Weapons.
Survival Hunter Best in Slot (BiS) Gear in MoP Classic (Pre-Raid)
The best weapons you can find pre-raid in the first phase are:

Klalith, Fangs of the Swarm, Raigonn, Gate of the Setting Sun.
Amber Sprayer of Klaxxi’vess, The Klaxxi, Exalted.
Tempestuous Longbow, Siege of Niuzao Temple, General Pa’valak.

The best pre-raid trinkets are:

Relic of Xuen if you have Inscription.
Searing Words, Darkmaster Gandling, Scholomance.
Windswept Pages, from Temple of the Jade Serpent.
