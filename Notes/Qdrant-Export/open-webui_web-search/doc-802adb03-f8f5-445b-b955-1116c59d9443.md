Naramsin’s reign, a governor of Susa had a statue of a goddess made whose 
throne is adorned by lions, each of which has a rosette on his shoulder. The 
only other shoulder ornament so far known in the third millennium B. C. 
dates from about the same time as our cylinder. It is found on lions in the 
chapel of an Egyptian queen 4 who was married to two kings of the Sixth 
Dynasty, Merenre and Pepi II. The death of Merenre may have occurred
