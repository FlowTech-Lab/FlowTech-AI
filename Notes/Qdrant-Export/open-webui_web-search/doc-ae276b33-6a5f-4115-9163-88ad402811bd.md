Glyph of Pathfinding: Faster movement on foot and mount. Time saved is XP gained.


Glyph of Mending: Makes it easier to keep your pet healthy between fights.


Glyph of Camouflage: Lets you move while stealthed at level 85+. Great for avoiding mobs or sneaking to objectives.


Minor Glyphs:


Glyph of Fetch: Your pet loots for you. Don’t sleep on this—it’s a huge time-saver.


Glyph of Aspect of the Cheetah: Removes the daze penalty. Now you can run freely without getting slowed.


Glyph of Revive Pet: Faster pet res. Essential for emergencies when your pet face-checks death.


Best Pet Specialization for Leveling
Set your pet to Tenacity while leveling. Here’s why:


Tenacity: Tank spec. More survivability, better aggro, and you don’t have to babysit your pet constantly.


Ferocity: Great in groups or dungeons, but your pet will die more often in solo play.


Cunning: PvP focused. Not really helpful for leveling unless you're constantly fighting other players.
