When we turn to Rosen 2 seal (Fig. 3), other fragments of seal imagery 
found at Konar Sandal South allow us to anchor that seal as well to the 
region. What is most characteristic of this seal is the modeled, almost 
sculptural character of the large figures. While the composition is dense, it 
is not structured in informal horizontal registers, but is rather organized into 
triangular zones. The modeled style is obvious in the bodies of the two 
male figures that raise their heavily muscled arms high in the air. But it is 
even more obvious in the features of their faces. Each has a heavy, short 
beard that is overlapped by a long mustache; each has a prominent nose 
with flaring nostrils; and each has an eye that, as observed by Porada, is 
defined by a horizontal line rather than a drilling. The body of the dragon is
