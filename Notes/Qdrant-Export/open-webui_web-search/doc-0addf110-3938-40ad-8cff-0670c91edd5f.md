Cancelaura Macro
This macro is very easy to use and can function with most effects. You may choose to incorporate a /cast ability to toggle an effect and then a /cancelaura “spell”. Doing so lets you press the button once to activate the effect and press it a second time to cancel the effect instantly. You may also choose just to use the /cancelaura part to get rid of an effect you activated with another button.
/cast Deterrence/cancelaura Deterrence
