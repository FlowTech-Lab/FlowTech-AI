It must be noted, however, that even in the earlier designs figures of dei¬

The conclusion to be drawn from this evidence is that the style reached 
its height not in the time of Hammurabi but in the time of his grandfather 
Apil-Sin. 

As to the origins of this style, they should of course be sought earlier 
and, as Frankfort was the first to suggest, Isin and Larsa are the first places 
of which one thinks in this connection, “indeed older centers of culture than 
Babylon”. 20
