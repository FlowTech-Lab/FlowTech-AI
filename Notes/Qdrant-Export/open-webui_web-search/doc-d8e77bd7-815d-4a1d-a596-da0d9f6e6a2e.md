Survival Hunter Gems in MoP Classic
Put the following gems into your gear as a  Survival Hunter in MoP Classic:
Meta:  Capacitive Primal Diamond or  Agile Primal Diamond.
Red: Use one  Delicate Serpent's Eye as Jewelcrafter. Then  Wicked Vermilion Onyx or  Perfect Wicked Tiger Opal if not hit capped, otherwise  Delicate Primordial Ruby or  Perfect Delicate Pandarian Garnet.
Yellow:  Deadly Vermilion Onyx /  Perfect Deadly Tiger Opal.
Blue:  Rigid River's Heart or  Perfect Rigid Lapis Lazuli if not hit capped, otherwise  Glinting Imperial Amethyst or  Perfect Glinting Roguestone.
Use red gems in your prismatic gem slots.
