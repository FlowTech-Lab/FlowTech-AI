Level 25: Glyph of Fetch You’ll quickly come to appreciate not having to run up and loot every corpse, and it will save you a lot of time over the long run.
Level 50: Glyph of Aspect of the Cheetah You’ll be free to leave your Aspect of the Cheetah active as you run around the world, without fear of being dazed by any random mobs you run past.
Level 75: Glyph of Tame Beast/Glyph of Revive Pet Any time you’re planning to tame a new pet, you can use a glyph to make the process easier. The rest of the time you can glyph your Revive Pet spell to make it easier to resurrect your pet when you need to.
