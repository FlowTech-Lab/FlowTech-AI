Hunters can equip Cloth and Leather from level 1, and they can use Mail starting at level 40. It you use Mail heirlooms, they will automatically be treated as Leather for the first 40 levels. From level 50 on, you will always want to use Mail Armor due to your Mail Specialization passive ability, which gives a 5% buff to your most important stat. Before that, feel free to wear whatever gives you the best stats. Hunters can’t use Shields.
Hunters can use many kinds of weapons, but ranged weapons are required for some of their abilities
