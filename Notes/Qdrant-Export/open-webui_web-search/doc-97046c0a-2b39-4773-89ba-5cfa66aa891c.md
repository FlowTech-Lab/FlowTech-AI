P.0.W. x2 (Early Season, costs 2 Sparks) — This
item is not our long-term BiS, but it is a very strong early Season
option. It is not as good as the 2x  Dawnthread Lining setup in the long
run. The second boss on Mythic difficulty will drop a better Weapon, and you
can get better weapons from the Great Vault. Crafting a Weapon does
have some risk factor to it for this reason.
(BiS, 1 Spark) —  Glyph-Etched Gauntlets with  Dawnthread Lining — Note that if Crafting this right away will mess with your Tier Set acquisition, you may want to go for Bracers first. Note that as  Dark Ranger, you can use  Duskthread Lining instead, which grants Versatility and will often sim similarly to Dawn while offering damage reduction.
(BiS, 1 Spark) —  Glyph-Etched Vambraces with  Dawnthread Lining. Note that as  Dark Ranger, you can use  Duskthread Lining instead, which grants Versatility and will often sim similarly to Dawn while offering damage reduction.
