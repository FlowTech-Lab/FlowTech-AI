Some DNS fun
I've also been cleaning up some dns configuration:

We used to list ns-iad01 and ns-iad02 in our glue records, but listing
a datacenter there seems not great to me, especially since we are no
longer in iad2. :) So, We now just have ns01 and ns03. This also included
our NS and SOA records.
We also now have ipv6 at our new datacenter, so I got our registrar to
add ipv6 records for those servers. (all our nameservers now have ipv6
addresses).
One of the vm's moved above was ns02, so I got the regisrar to update
that one with the new addresses.
We still had 3 zones that were getting signed by SHA1 dnssec keys.
We had dropped the actual glue records for those, but they were
still being created. I dropped those and now there should be 100%
no sha1 left on our control host. :)
We had some more leftover iad2 zones and some phx2 (!) zones.
I dropped most of those, but there's a few that will need to wait
until after the beta freeze as they are in our named.conf.
