Best Hunter Specs for Leveling
Hunters have three distinct specializations, but not all are created equal for the leveling grind:


Beast Mastery (BM): The best leveling spec by far. Your pet becomes a near-invincible tank while your damage comes online early. Great for solo play and handling multiple mobs.


Marksmanship (MM): Offers big crits and strong damage later in the game. However, it lacks early efficiency and requires more gear to feel smooth.


Survival (SV): A DoT and trap-focused spec. It’s fun, but its damage-over-time playstyle makes it slower for leveling compared to BM.


Hunter Leveling Builds
In MoP Classic, talents are chosen tier by tier, offering flexibility. For leveling, you want talents that keep your pet alive and improve your sustained damage. A common BM leveling setup looks like this:


Level 15: Dire Beast – Extra pet damage and focus generation.
