8.2.  Dark Ranger Hero Talent Tier Set Bonus for Beast Mastery Hunter

 Hunter Dark Ranger 11.2 Class Set 2pc —  Black Arrow damage
increased by 10%,  Bleak Powder damage increased by 20%, and the
cooldown of  Call of the Wild is reduced by 60 sec.
 Hunter Dark Ranger 11.2 Class Set 4pc —  Withering Fire's
damage increased by 50%, and whenever you consume  Deathblow, you have
a 50% chance to cause all  Withering Fire  Black Arrow barrages
in your next  Call of the Wild to fire 1 additional  Black Arrow.
