The Repertoire was an early creation of Gardin’s, the direction of which 
he passed on to Frangoise Digard. But the idea and the method had been 
worked out in some detail before the project was set in motion. The work 
consists in an analysis of the representations on cylinder seals for the pur¬ 
pose of deriving comparanda from the computerized items. Over 4,600 
cylinders are reproduced with photographs or drawings on cards, sizes 
7.50 x 12.50 cm, which fill two boxes. The cards are exactly of the right 
size and consistency for easy handling, which is an achievement worth 
mentioning. The code for the computer is contained in Volume 2 and an 
explanation of the code’s terminology appears in Volume 3. The introduc¬ 
tion and some of the results of the work with the Repertoire are found in 
Volume 1. In the catalogue are assembled seals from small collections and
