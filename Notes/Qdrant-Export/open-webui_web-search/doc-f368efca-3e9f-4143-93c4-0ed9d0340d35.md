Chapter 1 - In The BeginningIn the beginning, there was the word, and the word was . . . ‘And.’ 
Wait . . . that’s not very impressive. Or accurate, really. Let’s try again. 
In the beginning, there was the sentence, and the sentence was . . . ‘And which god would you like to be?’
‘Excuse me?’ he – or perhaps just he – asked.
The Other stared back at him above flat-rimmed spectacles. ‘God. You. Which. Like.’
It took a moment for Him – who was now distinctly feeling like just him – to understand that the combination of tone, facial expression (especially the bit with the spectacles), and word choice were signifiers of someone being patronizing.
‘Look, I don’t see any need to be rude, I’m new at this, you know?’ 
‘New at what?’ 
he – for he was now quite sure that his insignificance violated even the rules of capitalisation – threw up his hands. ‘Existence.’
