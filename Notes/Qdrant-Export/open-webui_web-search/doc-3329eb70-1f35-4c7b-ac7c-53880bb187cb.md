Survival

Maintain your Aspect of the Hawk buff at all times (level 12+).
Use Hunter’s Mark to apply the debuff to your main target (level 14+).
Use Serpent Sting to apply its debuff.
Use Explosive Shot on cooldown.
Use Kill Shot if it is available (level 35+).
Use Black Arrow on cooldown to apply and maintain its debuff if you expect the target to live for the full duration (level 50+).
Use Multi-Shot if you have enough Focus and you are fighting more than one target (level 24+).
Use Explosive Trap if it will hit more than one target (level 38+).
Use Arcane Shot if you have extra Focus.
Use Cobra Shot to generate Focus (level 81+).
Use Steady Shot until you get Cobra Shot.
