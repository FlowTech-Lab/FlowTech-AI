Of fifty-six cylinders of Mitannian style found at Beisan the following can be assigned to 
Group ii of the Nuzi imprints, Iraq xi. 35, 37, 39, 41-44, 46, 50, 53, 56, 60-65, 69-70, 71-73, 
76, 82, 87, 88, 115. To these could be added a number for which there were no parallels 
among the impressions of Group ii of Nuzi. With the exception of Iraq xi, no. 35 and perhaps 
nos. 37 and 88 (the latter badly preserved) which have the precise and clean carving noted in 
the cylinders Alalakh nos. 25-27 (see n. 20), the cylinders from Beisan here listed all have 
simple, large, and rather sloppily cut (or moulded) designs, in which there may be noted a 
preference for rows of animals or fish and for vertical and sometimes horizontal panels filled 
with various patterns, usually a star impaled on a vertical axis (e.g. Iraq xi, nos. 43, 44). This
