At present there is no reason to reject the general assumption that the 
man represented on cylinder seals being borne by an eagle as in PL I A is 
indeed Etana. His posture resembles that of a child sitting on its mother's 
arm, 9 which suggests both the consent and confidence engendered in Etana 
by the eagle's speech quoted above.
