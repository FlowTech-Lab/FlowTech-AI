The last feature to be engraved in No. 1 was probably the figures’ feet, 
beginning at the left and ending with the seated god whose feet had to be 
severely shortened in order to retain the necessary interval between his feet. 
This shows how important such intervals, which assured the clarity and 
rhythm of the composition, seemed to the engraver.
