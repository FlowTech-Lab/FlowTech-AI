Best Race for Hunters in MoP
The best race for a  Hunter depends on whether you are playing on the Alliance or Horde side:
 Alliance
In PvE,  Pandaren is the best choice for  Hunters on Alliance side due to  Epicurean.
 Worgen is the second-best race picks on Alliance side due to  Viciousness.
In PvP,  Human is the best choice for Alliance  Hunters, followed by  Worgen.
 Horde
In PvE,  Orc is the best race for  Hunters on Horde side due to  Blood Fury.
It is closely followed by  Troll due to  Berserking.
In PvP,  Orc is the best choice for Horde  Hunters.
