Glyphs for Survival Hunter
Glyphs customize your abilities according to their specifications and improve the in-game experiences by altering the game mechanics to a certain extent. Based on the specifications of each glyph, they are classified as major or minor ones.
Major Glyphs:


Glyph of Animal Bond: Increases the amount of healing received when a pet is active. Very useful when having a pet as it passively increases the HP.



Glyph of Deterrence: Adds utility to the defensive cooldown and helps cooldown quicker.



Glyph of Pathfinding: This massively boosts movement speed when mounted and is one of the best glyphs, as you can navigate faster.


Minor Glyphs:


Glyph of Lesser Proportion: This Glyph shrinks pet size, which is not very useful compared to the other glyphs.



Glyph of Revive Pet: This Glyph makes the Pet revive uninterruptible. So, when you are reviving your pet, you cannot be interrupted by any enemy intervention.
