Level 15: Posthaste Mobility is king for leveling, and this gives you a little more. Narrow Escape and Crouching Tiger, Hidden Chimera can be useful for defense, but hopefully you won’t be needing them too much while leveling.
Level 30: Binding Shot A strong crowd control on a short-ish cooldown, great to throw out if you accidentally over-pull. Wyvern Sting is more situational and Intimidation only stuns one target on a longer cooldown.
Level 45: Spirit Bond This is a nice steady passive healing effect that will keep you chain pulling mobs. Exhilaration is also a strong choice if you prefer an on-demand healing cooldown instead – just remember that you have to actually use it for it to have any value. Aspect of the Iron Hawk is not as useful in solo content, particularly since it doesn’t affect your pet at all.
