with that article, she put the world of southeastern Iran on the map and 
started the gradual accumulation of evidence for what we can now 
confidently identify as an independent, indigenous Bronze Age civilization 
in southeastern Iran centered in the province of Kerman and the valley of 
the Halil River. The 1960’s and 70’s excavations at Tepe Yahya and 
Shahdad brought to light a dozen or so seals that established with 
confidence the existence of an indigenous glyptic tradition in the region. In 
1975 she described the close relationship of the Foroughi seal to those from 
Tepe Yahya and especially from Shahdad where seals made from the same 
white stone had been carved in an almost identical style (Porada 1975a).
