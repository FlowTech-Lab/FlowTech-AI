This is your standard BM Hunter Raiding Talent Build for single target. Most of the choices can be swapped around depending on the fights and the utility requirements, so here’s a quick explanation on how each talent can be changed.
Crouching Tiger, Hidden Chimera increases your mobility but if you are at risk of being snared, slowed, or need a better instant burst of speed, Posthaste is a better choice. Binding shot is just great all around, Aspect of the Iron Hawk gives an incredible 10% DR but if you need the burst healing you can pick Exhilaration.
Dire Beast is the best single target choice but if there is a lot of cleave involved, you can take Thrill of the hunt.
And finally, swap Glaive Toss for Barrage in fights with a lot of adds.
Best Beast Mastery Hunter Raid Glyphs
Regarding Major Glyphs, you will want to obtain Glyph of Deterrence, Glyph of Animal Bond and Glyph of Disengage. Other options are Glyph of Ice trap, Glyph of Liberation, Glyph of Endless Wrath.
