Joan Aruz is Curator in Charge of the Department of Ancient Near Eastern Art at 
The Metropolitan Museum of Art, where she has worked for the last twenty- 
five years. During that time she has written many articles on seals and intercul- 
tural exchange and has mounted a number of special exhibitions. Among them 
are Art of the First Cities: The Third Millennium B.C. from the Mediterranean 
to the Indus (2003) and Beyond Babylon: Art, Trade and Diplomacy in the Se¬ 
cond Millennium B.C. (2008), both with extensive catalogues. These show, like 
the one she is preparing for Fall 2014 with the title Assyria to Iberia at the 
Dawn of the Classsical Age, focus on cross-cultural encounters and their impact 
on the visual arts. Dr. Aruz pursued her doctoral studies, with Edith Porada as 
an advisor and mentor, and her 1986 dissertation was published in 2008 as 
Marks of Distinction: Seals and Cultural Exchange Between the Aegean and 
the Orient (ca. 2600 - 1360 B.C.).
