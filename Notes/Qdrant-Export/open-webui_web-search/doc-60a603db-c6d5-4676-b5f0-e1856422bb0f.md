Spirit Bond✅
A decently strong option for survivability, as long as you don’t take consistent AoE splash damage. In terms of healing, this is by far the most potent talent that you can acquire, but it can’t shield you from consistent high amounts of damage. Aspect of the Iron Hawk performs better in most cases where near one-shot mechanics are involved, but Spirit Bond is a decent contender as long as your pet remains alive.
