There can be no doubt, however, that the extent of Tukulti-Ninurta’s 
westward reach is reflected in the glyptic record. A seal found at Tyre was 
dated by Porada to his reign and judged to be an import from a provincial 
Assyrian town rather than a local product. 22 Her view is supported by the 
work of Dominique Collon on a seal from Tell Mohammed Arab. 23 Tally 
Oman, however, has focused attention, not on the typically Middle 
Assyrian aspects of the imagery on the Tyre cylinder, but rather its unusual 
features and comparisons with an heirloom discovered in Beer-Sheba. She 
has suggested that the long-haired divinity with a lightning bolt on the Tyre 
seal was inspired by North Syrian or Hittite renderings of the storm-god. 24
