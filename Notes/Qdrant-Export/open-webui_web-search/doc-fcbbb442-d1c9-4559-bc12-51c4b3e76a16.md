Talent Build
Much like the other two specializations, Beast Mastery Hunter has the option of diversifying its Talent Build based on the encounter! The best examples would be how Glaive Toss is the primary Single-Target talent of the build, while Barrage is the main AoE tool. Other examples are Posthaste and Crouching Tiger, Hidden Chimera, where one is considerably better for encounters that require extra movement speed while the other is better for consistent repositioning.
Talents such as Dire Beast, Fervor, A Murder of Crows, and Blink Strikes are categorized by the best DPS generator, but they also depend on the player’s playstyle and how well they can fit those talents into their rotation.
