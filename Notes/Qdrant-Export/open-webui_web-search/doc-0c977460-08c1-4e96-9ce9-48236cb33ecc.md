The Hunter knows the wilderness like the back of their hand, and can navigate the open world better than any other class in World of Warcraft. With a trusty beast companion at their side, plenty of tools for tracking and trapping, and three different damage dealing builds to choose from, no foe is too great a challenge. As a result, Hunters are frequently seen as one of the strongest classes for solo questing and farming, a trend that continues in the Mists of Pandaria expansion.
This guide will help you choose a build, equipment, professions, rotation, and where to go as a Hunter to level from 1 to 90 as efficiently as possible in MoP Classic so you can focus on what you’re really here for: taming all of the coolest-looking beasts.
