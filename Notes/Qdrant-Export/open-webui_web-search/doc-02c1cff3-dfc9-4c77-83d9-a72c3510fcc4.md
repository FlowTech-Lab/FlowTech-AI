The Akkad period occupies a unique position in Mesopotamian art. It is 
the only phase in which we note a preoccupation with the natural appear¬ 
ance of man and beast. In our cylinder seal, for example, the beauty of the 
strong nude male body in action seems to have been fully appreciated and 
rendered without the exaggerations found in Assyrian art of a later time.
