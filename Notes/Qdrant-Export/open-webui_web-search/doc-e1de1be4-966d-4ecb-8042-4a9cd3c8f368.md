The final article concerning Edith Porada's dissertation depicts the seals, which she 
had analysed, that are not shown in R. M. Boehmer's Die Entwicklung der Glyptik 
wahrend der Akkad-Zeit (1965). Read together with Boehmer, this review of her first 
academic work provides an extensive presentation of Akkad period seals. Her list of 
iconographic motifs is especially valuable. This book will thus be of didactical use 
for any introductory course on Ancient Near Eastern seals.
