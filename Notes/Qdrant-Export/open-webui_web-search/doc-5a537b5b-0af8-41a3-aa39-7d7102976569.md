Posthaste✅
The most used talent from the first tier, Posthaste, often provides invaluable movement speed in dungeon and raid environments. It is generally taken as a default option, but it is sometimes swapped for Crouching Tiger, Hidden Chimera for cases where you must soak more damage.
Narrow Escape❌
This talent is a PvP option and not suited for PvE environments. It can be used in PvE for its strong snare effect, but the lack of mobility simply makes the other two options far more valuable.
Crouching Tiger, Hidden Chimera✅
A popular option for difficult encounters with a lot of splash AoE, Crouching Tiger, Hidden Chimera is usually taken for its Deterrence cooldown reduction element. While it also provides mobility by reducing Disengage‘s cooldown, Posthaste is overall better for improved mobility.
