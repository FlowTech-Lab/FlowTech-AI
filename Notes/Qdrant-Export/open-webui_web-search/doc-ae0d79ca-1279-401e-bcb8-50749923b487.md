Reprinted from: Journal of Cuneiform Studies 4, 1950, 155-162. 


Critical Review: 

Corpus of Ancient Near Eastern Seals in North American 
Collections, Volume I: The Collection of the Pierpont 
Morgan Library Catalogued and Edited by Edith Porada in 
Collaboration with Briggs Buchanan (Bollingen Series 
XIV). New York, Pantheon Books, 1949 


The editor of the Journal of Cuneiform Studies kindly suggested to the 
author that she herself write a review of the first volume of the Corpus, thus 
giving her an opportunity to make the corrections and additions which have 
become necessary as a result of this work done since the completion of the 
volume.
