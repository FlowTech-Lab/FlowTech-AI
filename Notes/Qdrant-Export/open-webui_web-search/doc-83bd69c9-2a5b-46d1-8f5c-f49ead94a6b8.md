Strengths



Excellent burst damage thanks to  Stampede.Strong passive cleave with  Beast Cleave.Brings a raid buff with  Trueshot Aura.Strong utility, including unique abilities such as  Misdirection and  Feign Death.One of the easiest classes to gear early on.Incredible leveling speed and solo-play ability out in the world.





Weaknesses



Pet-based class, which means you will need to micro-manage your pet sometimes.Lackluster sustained single-target damage.Poor overall AOE damage.Least valuable Hunter specialization in raids.Lack of defensive and self-healing cooldowns.
