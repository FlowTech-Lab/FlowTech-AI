The highest ranking figure in this extraordinary seal is the seated one facing 
toward the left, receiving a bow and arrow from a facing seated human 
figure. This left facing figure is certainly divine to judge from his headgear 
which consists of the head and neck of a horned bull emerging from his 
forehead. This headgear is identical to that worn by the enthroned divine 
figure seen on the Foroughi seal. Surely this image denotes a paramount 
deity in the Halil River Pantheon. Another version of this headgear is 
represented as the pair of horns without the bull’s head emerging from the 
forehead. On the same reconstructed seal from Konar Sandal South, one of 
the heroic figures standing to the side of the seated pair has a pair of horns 
similarly emerging from his forehead.
