not precisely paralleled in any Middle Assyrian representations of deities. 
In the sealings made with a royal cylinder of the Middle Assyrian period on 
the tablets of the vassal treaties of Esarhaddon found at Nimrud, Fig. 7, one 
of the two large figures of deities shown is, however, a god who holds in 
one hand a lightning symbol that differs from the lightning fork held by the 
god on the cylinder from Tyre but doubtless was intended to convey the 
same meaning. In the other hand the god in Fig. 7 holds an emblem which
