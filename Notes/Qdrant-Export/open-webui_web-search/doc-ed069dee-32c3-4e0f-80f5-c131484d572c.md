Interloper's Chain Boots - Boots from The Soul Hunters.
  These simply increase the proc rate of your Cloak and are always BiS. Do not craft Boots, you will want these even from lower difficulties if you can.
 Band of the Shattered Soul - Ring from Dimensius The All Devouring, the final boss. Gives you occasional Cosmic damage reduction. This is a good Ring in general due to its high Haste, the extra benefit is quite subtle and not a bonus that you will explicitly chase this Ring for alone.
