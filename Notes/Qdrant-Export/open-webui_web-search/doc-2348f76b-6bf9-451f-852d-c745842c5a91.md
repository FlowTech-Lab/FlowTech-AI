we again have to turn to the cylinder seals for such meager information as 
might be gleaned from them. On Pl. VIII, 2 a worshipper approaches the 
warrior with an offering in the same |62] manner as he is seen elsewhere 
bringing a kid to a deity. 24 In other instances a female figure stands before 
the warrior raising her hand in a gesture of worship or prayer. 25 Thus it 
follows that a figure who is being worshipped like a god may be counted 
among the deities. The warrior’s relation to a superior, perhaps the supreme
