Your pet tanks for you. You deal damage from afar while your beast holds aggro.


Exceptional mobility. With skills like Disengage and Aspect of the Cheetah, you’re always moving.


Tons of utility. Feign Death, Traps, and Camouflage help you avoid fights or reset tough encounters.


Great kiting potential. You rarely take damage if you play smart.


Weaknesses:


Pet micromanagement. You need to keep your pet happy, alive, and set to the right specialization.


No role flexibility. You're locked into DPS, so don’t expect fast dungeon queues as a tank or healer.


Limited self-healing. You rely on avoiding damage rather than mitigating or healing through it.


Best Hunter Spec for Leveling
While all three Hunter specs are viable in MoP Classic, Beast Mastery (BM) is far and away the best choice for leveling.


Beast Mastery: The king of solo leveling. Your pet becomes a true tank, and your damage revolves around supporting it. It’s reliable, safe, and fast.
