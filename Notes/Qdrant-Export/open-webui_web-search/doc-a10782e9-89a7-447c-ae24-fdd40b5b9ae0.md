“Syrian” Cylinders 

The cylinder (Fig. 1) said by Contenau to belong to Syrian glyptic art (Tresor 
de Tod, p. 16, s.v. pi. XL, 15226 bis), actually has its closest parallel in a cylin¬ 
der found by K. Kenyon at Jericho in tomb A 1279 (Fig. a), 9 dated by A. Ben- 
Tor in the beginning of the Early Bronze II period, probably contemporary with 
the second half of the First Egyptian Dynasty. Ben-Tor included the seal in a 
group that he cited as related to the Jamdat Nasr type seals of the Diyala re¬ 
gion. 10 K. Kenyon published the cylinder as a bead, which may be correct in 
view of its purely ornamental character.
