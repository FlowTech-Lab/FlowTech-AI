Several Cypriote cylinders have the same smooth bodied animal forms 
with sharp cuts indicating the details. One in the British Museum (PI. XX: 
2) is very monotonous, showing two spread eagles over two recumbent 
goats. Somewhat more variety is introduced in the upper register of another 
cylinder of the same group (PI. XX: 3) in the Cyprus Museum. An example 
in the Erlenmeyer Collection (PI. XX: 4) has the lion bending over the bull 
in a very realistic rendering of a posture also portrayed on an Agate lentoid 
from Argos 20 found with two vessels of Late Helladic IIIA. Closest to the 
cylinder from Melissa in style and accomplishment is one in the Louvre 
(PI. XX: 5), which was discussed by Furumark, J. F. Daniel, and Olivier
