Erika Bleibtreu thought it too complicated for the reader to look up the 
many photos of seals from the dissertation in the section of plates. There¬ 
fore, this article exceptionally gives both drawings and photos together 
with the text and is filed at the end of our book in order to be printed on 
photographic paper as the plates. The photos and drawings are taken in 
most cases from the publications, which were used by Edith Porada in her 
dissertation, written from 1933 until 1934. All quotations from Porada’s 
works are abbreviated according to the year of publication given in the bib¬ 
liography at the beginning of this book.
