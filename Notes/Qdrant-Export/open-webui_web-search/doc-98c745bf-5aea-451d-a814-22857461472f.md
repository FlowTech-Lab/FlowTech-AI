Marksmanship

Maintain your Aspect of the Hawk buff at all times (level 12+).
Use Hunter’s Mark to apply the debuff to your main target (level 14+).
Use Serpent Sting to apply its debuff.
Use Steady Shot twice in a row to apply and maintain the Steady Focus buff on yourself (level 63+).
Use Multi-Shot whenever you have enough Focus if you are fighting 4 or more targets (level 24+).
Use Chimera Shot on cooldown (level 60+).
Use Aimed Shot when you have 3 charges of Master Marksman (level 58+).
Use Kill Shot if it is available (level 35+).
Use Aimed Shot if you have extra Focus.
Use Steady Shot to generate Focus.
