Exhilaration is a new healing cooldown, which heals you and your pet
for a substantial amount on a 2-minute cooldown. This is a nice button to have, but simply
not as strong as the other two options.
 Aspect of the Iron Hawk is an upgraded version of  Aspect of the Hawk,
with a flat 10% damage reduction built in. This is an absolutely incredible defensive layer
passively built in for hunters, which is a relatively weak class defensively. 
 Spirit Bond causes you and your pet to passively regenerate 3% of your
health every 2 seconds at all times. This is a nice passive regeneration option, but as long
as you have food or bandages available, is largely not as useful.
