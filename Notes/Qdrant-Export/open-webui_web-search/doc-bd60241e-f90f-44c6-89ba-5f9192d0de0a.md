Level 90 – Tier VI

Glaive Toss✅
This is the default Single-Target talent choice from the sixth tier, providing the best value! The ability is also potent in Cleave scenarios where 2 or 4 targets are involved. Since it has a low cooldown and Focus cost, you will spam this on repeat whenever it becomes available.
Powershot❌
This talent is sadly one of the worst choices that you can opt for due to its positioning requirements. While the Weapon Damage it deals is fairly high, it is completely outclassed by Barrage in AoE and Glaive Toss in Single-Target. The reason for this is not only its weird positioning requirements, but also its high cooldown, which makes it sub-optimal. 
Barrage✅
The default AoE option from the sixth tier, Barrage, provides the single strongest AoE spell in your kit, hitting an endless number of targets during its channel. This ability is particularly useful in AoE Cleave comps, allowing you to clear dungeons very quickly and devastate mob groups in raids.
