Finally it is useful to discuss briefly the use of the more Mesopotamian 
horned headdress of divinity in the glyptic of the Halil River Basin. The 
goddess on the back of the dragon figure on Rosen 2 has a curved horn 
emerging from each side of her head. This mode of denoting divinity 
appears on seals in Mesopotamia in the Early Dynastic period at the same 
time as it is used at Konar Sandal South, most frequently on female figures 
who are associated with fertility (e.g. Amiet 1980: 1356, 1357). Among the
