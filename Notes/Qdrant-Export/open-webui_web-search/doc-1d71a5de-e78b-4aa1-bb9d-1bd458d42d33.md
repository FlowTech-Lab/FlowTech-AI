No more melee weapons.
Wing clip, Parry, Raptor Strike and Aspect of the wild are deleted.
No more range limitation. You can use your ranged attacks even in melee.
Ranged attacks can be dodged, so you need a bit of Expertise.
Rapid Fire, Bestial Wrath now have a reduced cooldown.
Kill command has a 25-yard range.
Revive pet is way faster.
Beast cleave is a new passive, allowing your pet auto-attacks to cleave after Multishot.
Trueshot Aura is now unlocked for every spec.
Stampede is a new offensive cooldown, calling all your pets to attack your target.
No more talent trees for pets.
