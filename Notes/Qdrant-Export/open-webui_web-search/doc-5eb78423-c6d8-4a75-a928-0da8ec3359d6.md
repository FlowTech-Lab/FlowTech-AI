Use Mend Pet Constantly: It’s cheap and keeps your pet alive so you don’t waste time resummoning.


Efficient Rotation:- Early: Kill Command → Arcane Shot → Cobra Shot to regen focus.- At 81+: Add Kill Shot for huge execute damage.


Pull Smart: Hunters excel at chain-pulling. Send your pet ahead while you loot, keeping momentum.


Traps for Control: Freezing Trap isn’t just PvP—it saves you if you overpull. Explosive Trap can also AoE down groups when farming.


Mount Early: Grab riding skills ASAP. Hunters level so quickly that walking feels like wasted time.
