km from Athens) where he used to dig for antiquities. More precise details 
as to the exact place of discovery, context, etc. are unknown. The cylinder 
is only mentioned in his notes, Fouilles de Vari. It is now in the possession 
of his daughter, Mrs. J. Serpieri. The cylinder was mentioned by Sir Arthur 
Evans in Palace of Minos IV, p. 409, and reproduced in a drawing, ibid. 
Fig. 339. The material is lapis lazuli, weathered to a lead-grey color. The

erased and a new design carved on the cylinder. Still later, two gold caps
