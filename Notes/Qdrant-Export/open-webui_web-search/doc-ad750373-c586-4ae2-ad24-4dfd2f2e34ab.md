The Theban Seals And Cyprus 

The island of Cyprus is the one region that was certainly involved in the 
collection and re-carving, as well as the creation, of the lapis lazuli seals 
that travelled to the Theban palace workshop. Among the most fascinating 
of the recarved seals was one that must have originally belonged to an Old 
Babylonian official. It depicts a presentation scene, with the procession of a 
bald headed male, followed by a suppliant goddess, toward an enthroned 
divinity. A Cypriot craftsman transformed the image by adding vertical 
wings to the male figure, exaggerated features to others, as well as a 
typically Cypriot scene of a winged figure holding two horned animals
