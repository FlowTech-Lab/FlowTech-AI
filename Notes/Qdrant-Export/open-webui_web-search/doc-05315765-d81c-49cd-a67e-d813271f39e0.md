Jade Forest (85-86): Introductory hub with heavy story beats and plenty of linear quests.



Valley of the Four Winds (86-87): Large open zones ideal for Hunters, with quests spread across farmland and easy mob pulls.



Kun-Lai Summit (87-88): Vertical terrain makes travel tricky, but later flying unlocks and streamlines the zone.



Townlong Steppes (88-89): Compact quest hubs with consistent XP gain.



Dread Wastes (89-90): Final push with denser mobs and the Klaxxi storyline. Expect a tougher but faster pace here.



Dungeons & Group Play
Hunters aren’t just questers; you’ll also dip into dungeons. Here are some important tips to keep in mind:


Queueing: You must run a dungeon once per level range for quests (e.g., Deadmines at 15–20, Scarlet Monastery at 30-40). After that, questing remains faster.


Role in Groups: You’re DPS. Use Freezing Trap for crowd control, and keep Misdirection handy to help tanks with threat.
