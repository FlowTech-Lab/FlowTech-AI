Level 30 – Tier II

Binding Shot✅
The most potent talent from this tier in terms of crowd control, allowing you to stun an unlimited number of foes if they attempt to exit the ability’s radius. This talent comes with a gimmick, as skilled hunters can place it anywhere, resulting in an instant stun, similar to the Intimidation talent.
Wyvern Sting✅
This talent is niche and often considered the worst option from this tier, as any damage source can instantly dispel it. However, it can be very valuable against mobs that can be put to sleep and represent a threat to raid or dungeon groups. As long as you can coordinate with your allies, this talent may be very useful, depending on the encounter. 
Intimidation❌
This is an underwhelming option when compared to Binding Shot since it only applies to a single target. You should avoid using this talent for PvE, since it is far better suited for PvP.
