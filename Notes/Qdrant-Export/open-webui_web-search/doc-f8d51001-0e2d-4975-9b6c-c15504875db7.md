The Hunter’s beast pets provide tons of utility and survivability. It’s almost like having a built-in leveling partner.
Tracking allows you to see all sorts of creatures on the minimap, which is immensely helpful for questing and farming in the open world.
You have plenty of mobility thanks to Disengage and Aspect of the Cheetah. You also get Camouflage at level 85, which gives you the option to sneak past mobs and avoid engaging altogether.
Hunters have some excellent crowd control options with their many traps and Concussive Shot. This combined with their mobility makes them masters of kiting who almost never get hit.
If all else fails, you can always Feign Death and start over.
