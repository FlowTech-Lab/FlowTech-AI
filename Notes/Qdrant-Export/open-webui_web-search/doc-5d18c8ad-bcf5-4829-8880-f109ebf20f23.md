stone. There is no reason to assume that the group originated in Syria, because 
the largest number of such seals found at one site comes from the Royal Ceme¬ 
tery of Ur, 12 where six examples were discovered. Moreover, a cylinder in the 
Iraq Museum (Fig. b), published by Amiet, 13 shows a typical Early Dynastic 
banqueting scene above a row of festoons a motif thereby tied firmly to a char¬ 
acteristic Mesopotamian theme.
