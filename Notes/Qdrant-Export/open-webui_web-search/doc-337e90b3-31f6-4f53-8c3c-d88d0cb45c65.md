before the release of Mists of Pandaria Classic. Be sure to check back as we get closer

1.1. Is Beast Mastery Hunter Viable in Mists of Pandaria Classic?
Absolutely! While Beast Mastery is not as strong as Survival, it scales quite well and
will eventually be a strong choice in raids. Even still, you could absolutely play Beast Mastery
at every point in Mists of Pandaria in all content without issues. Beast Mastery Hunters in particular
have solid single target with a very versatile toolkit, specifically with incredible passive cleave
thanks to  Beast Cleave. Beast Mastery also benefits the most from the new
Hunter cooldown  Stampede out of any specialization, making them extremely
potent for burst damage.
