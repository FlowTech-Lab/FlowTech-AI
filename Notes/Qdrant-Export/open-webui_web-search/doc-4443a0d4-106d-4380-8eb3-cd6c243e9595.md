Saint Ethalia-who-shares-all-sorrows, I swore silently, help me escape these mercenary thugs before they can haul me back to court and dump me in the duelling circle so that fox-faced lunatic who calls herself the Vixen can stick her blade through my heart before mine even leaves its scabbard!
They were herding me deeper and deeper into the narrow alleys of the Paupers’ Market, apparently determined to keep me from the Temple District where I might beg sanctuary for the night. Fortunately for me, I’d no intention of sleeping in a church tonight.
‘Run down your hole, Rabbit, run up to the sky!
Run a little faster, or else you’ll surely die!’ 
‘Coming through!’ I shouted to a pair of street-cleaners wrestling a stinking refuse cart across the street. Grinning in reply, they pushed all the harder to cut me off. No doubt they were hoping for a reward from my pursuers; a coward fleeing a lawful duel always means plenty of coin to go around for those who help bring him to justice.
