Always be in Stance of the Wise Serpent. 
Use Renewing Mist between pulls to generate Chi.
Use Blackout Kick to maintain the Serpent’s Zeal buff when you have at least 2 Chi.
Use Tiger Palm to maintain the Tiger Power buff when you have at least 1 Chi.
Use Blackout Kick when you have Muscle Memory and at least 2 Chi if you are fighting more than one enemy. (level 20+)
Use Tiger Palm when you have Muscle Memory and at least 1 Chi. (level 20+)
Use Touch of Death when you have 3 Chi and it’s available. (level 22+)
Use Chi Wave on cooldown (level 30+).
Use Spinning Crane Kick to generate Chi if you are fighting more than two enemies. (level 46+)
Use Expel Harm on cooldown to generate Chi if you are within 10 yards of an enemy.
Use Jab to generate Chi otherwise.
