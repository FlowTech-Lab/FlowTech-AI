On the Complexity of Style and Iconography 
in Some Groups of Cylinder Seals from Cyprus* 


In 1948 I had found it necessary to prove that some fine cylinder seals had 
actually been made in Cyprus 1 since most of the examples collected by 
Cesnola 2 and Ohnefalsch-Richter 3 belonged to the class of which Henri 
Frankfort said “a certain coarse type of seal ... seems to occur mainly in 
Cyprus and is therefore probably of local make.” 4
