The last feature which demands attention on Fig. 3 is the vic tim, a stag. 
In contrast to the kneeling or recumbent bull-calf of Fig. 1 [l50| and to the 
griffin in similar posture on Fig. 2, the stag of Fig. 3 is shown in a lively 
posture, one foreleg gracefully placed forward, the other raised with equal 
grace. The hindfeet are slid far apart, the head is turned backwards and the 
short tail raised.
