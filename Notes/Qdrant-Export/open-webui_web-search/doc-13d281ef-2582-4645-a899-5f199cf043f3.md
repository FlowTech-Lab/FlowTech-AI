Minor Glyphs

Glyph of Lesser Proportion
A very useful glyph for raid environments, allowing you to reduce the size of your pet and notice raid mechanics more easily!
Glyph of Revive Pet
A great glyph for reviving your pet in-combat, as it reduces all pushback suffered whilst casting Revive Pet. 
Glyph of Stampede
This glyph allows you to summon extra copies of your current pet, instead of the 4 other pets in your stable. It can be a potent gameplay enhancer if you have a very strong pet or if you don’t own more pets. 
Glyph of Tame Beast
A practical glyph that allows you to tame any beast faster! Very useful for acquiring dangerous pets.
