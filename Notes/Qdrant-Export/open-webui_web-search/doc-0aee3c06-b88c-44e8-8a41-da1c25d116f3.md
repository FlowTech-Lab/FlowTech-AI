Beast Mastery Hunter Opener
Regarding Openers, you want to place Hunter's Mark, put your traps (Snake Trap and Explosive Trap) before pull, cast Cobra shot 2 seconds before pull, use Bestial Wrath, Stampede, Rapid Fire and Rabid, Dire Beast, Kill Command, Glaive Toss, and continue with the single target rotation.
