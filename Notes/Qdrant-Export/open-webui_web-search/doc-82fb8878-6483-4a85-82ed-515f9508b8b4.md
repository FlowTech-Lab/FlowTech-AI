Talent Builds

The Hunter is a pure DPS class with three different specializations focused on dealing damage. The minimum range has been removed from ranged weapons in Mists of Pandaria, but Hunters will still find it beneficial to keep their targets at a safe distance most of the time. The  Beast Mastery specialization excels in solo content due to its emphasis on your ever-useful beast companion, and it remains the recommended leveling build.  Marksmanship is the quintessential sniper spec and provides the strongest burst damage, which can be very satisfying in the open world.  Survival focuses more on utility and crowd control, which are not as effective for simply plowing through quests and mobs, although they can be useful against tougher enemies and in PvP.
