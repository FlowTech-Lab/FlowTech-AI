MoP Classic Beast Mastery Hunter Stat Priority
Reach your Hit and Expertise cap, so around 7.5% for both. Then, Agility is your best stat. As soon as a gear piece gives you more Agility, it’s a good idea to equip it.
After that, it goes: Critical Strike, Mastery and then Haste. Note that for Beast Mastery Hunters, secondary stats balance isn’t as important, most of the time a higher item level item is the best choice regardless of secondary stats.
