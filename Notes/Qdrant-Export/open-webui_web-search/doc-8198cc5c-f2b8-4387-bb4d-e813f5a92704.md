Conclusion
Leveling a Hunter in Mists of Pandaria Classic is one of the smoothest experiences WOW has to offer. If you have a strong pet that has ranged firepower and is self-sustained, you can handle almost any challenge throughout the game.
By the time you hit 90, besides mastering your Hunter toolkit, you will also set yourself up for dungeons, raids, or PvP. Add professions like Skinning or gathering for gold, and you’re fully prepped for endgame.
