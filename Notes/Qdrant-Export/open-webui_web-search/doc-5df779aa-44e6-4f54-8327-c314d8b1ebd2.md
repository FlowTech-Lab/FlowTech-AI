The SV Hunter dungeon talent build is very similar to the raid talent build and follows the same logic, so as expected: Barrage and Thrill of the Hunt for more AoE potential and exploding packs. The utility options follow the same reasoning, change them depending on the dungeon.
Best Survival Hunter Dungeon Glyphs
Regarding Major Glyphs, you will want to obtain Glyph of Deterrence, Glyph of Animal Bond and Glyph of Disengage. Other options are Glyph of Ice trap, Glyph of Liberation.
There are very few good Minor Glyphs, the ones worth noting are Glyph of Direction, Glyph of Fetch, Glyph of Aspect of the Cheetah, Glyph of Aspect of the Pack.
