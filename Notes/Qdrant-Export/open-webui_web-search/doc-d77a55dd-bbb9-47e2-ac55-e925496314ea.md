There are a few key points in the talent tree:
Unleashed Fury at level 21, increasing the damage your pet deals.Ferocity at level 26, increasing the critical chance of your pet.Intimidation at level 30, an on-demand stun and threat boost for your pet that goes a long way towards making sure your pet holds aggro.Bestial Wrath at level 40, a very strong 2-minute DPS cooldown for your petThe Beast Within at level 50, making Bestial Wrath also buff your damage.Invigoration at level 51, giving you mana back whenever your pet deals a critical hit.Beast Mastery at level 60, allowing you to tame “Exotic Pets” like Devilsaurs and Rhinos.Aimed Shot at level 71, a very strong instant damage spell.
A lot of players opt to skip Beast Mastery because exotic pets aren’t necessarily the best pets for leveling. You can skip it to go into Marksmanship earlier if you’d like, I just think they’re neat.
