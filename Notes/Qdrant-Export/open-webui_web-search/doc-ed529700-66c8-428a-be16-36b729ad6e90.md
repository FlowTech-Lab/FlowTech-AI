Pet Specialization

In Mists of Pandaria, any pet can be set to use one of three specializations. Tenacity is the tanking specialization and is recommended for leveling and other solo content, as it will make your pet sturdier and better able to hold aggro while you deal damage from range. Ferocity is the best specialization for dealing damage in group content where your pet does not need the extra tankiness. Cunning is best used in PvP to give your pet some extra mobility and to give yourself some extra defense.
