The collection of 428 cylinder and stamp seals ranging from the Proto- 
Literate period to Sasanian times, was a gift to the State of Israel by the 
Hahn-Voss family. Mr. and Mrs. Hahn of Rio de Janeiro and Mr. and Mrs. 
Peter Hahn, Surrey, England, as well as Mr. and Mrs. John Emerson Hahn, 
Los Angeles, were helpful in bringing to Jerusalem the collection of their 
sister and sister-in-law, the late Anne-Marie Hahn-Voss. Professor D.E. 
Wolff completed the legal formalities in connection with this donation. 
Professor Tallay Oman of the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, until 2007 
Rodney E. Soher Curator of Western Asiatic Antiquities, remembers a 
Hahn family member who visited the Israel Museum in the early 1990s 
telling her that the collection arrived via France as diplomatic mail.
