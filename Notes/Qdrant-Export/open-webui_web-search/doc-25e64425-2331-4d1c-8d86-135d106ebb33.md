Battles of the gods 

In the contest formula human-form gods replace the vanquisher and the 
attacked. A scene combining hero-animal contest and combating gods also 
exists. 21 The groups may be pairs of gods or a triple-figure group, where 
two gods attack a third. The vanquishing gods are often rayed.
