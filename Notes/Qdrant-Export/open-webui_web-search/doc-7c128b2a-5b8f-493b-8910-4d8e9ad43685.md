The inscription contains a personal name, Ur- d Pa-bil-sag, servant of the 
god Pabilsag. 1 The large cuneiform signs and awkward insertion of the 
column in the design, which otherwise shows such exquisite feeling for 
balance of forms, suggests that a secondary seal cutter was charged with
