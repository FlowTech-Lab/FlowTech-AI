P. di Cesnola, Salaminia, London 1882, Pl. XII, 16, although the seal now in the Ash¬

minia, Pl. XIII, 18 and 24, belong to the same group, as does Salaminia, Pl. XIII, 17 
where the central figure differs from those of the other seals by being enthroned. If these 
seals were really found in or around ancient Salamis, they must have come from Enko¬ 
mi. See on this question Masson, op. cit., in note 13, above, p. 34. The place of discov¬ 
ery in Cyprus of two other seals in the Ashmolean Museum, Nos. 975, 976, both belong¬ 
ing to this group, is unknown.
