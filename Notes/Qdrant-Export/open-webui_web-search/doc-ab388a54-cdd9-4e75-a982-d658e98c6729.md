Fervor✅
Considered the best for the other Hunter specializations, Fervor is actually not the favorite in this tier for Beast Mastery Hunter. While it can provide great Focus regeneration, especially for openers, Dire Beast is considered the superior option in terms of raw DPS. Beginner hunters may choose this talent to get more accustomed to openers, especially when undergeared. 
Dire Beast✅
Dire Beast is the most potent talent from this tier for Beast Mastery Hunters in terms of Single-Target encounters! Because the summoned beast also counts as a Pet, your Mastery enhances all the damage it deals. While it doesn’t provide the same burst of Focus as Fervor, the overall value it grants is fairly similar at the end of the ability.
Thrill of the Hunt✅
This option is usually avoided for any Single-Target encounter and is only prioritized for AoE encounters. While RNG is heavily involved, the effect can trigger back-to-back, resulting in some devastating effects and high DPS output.
