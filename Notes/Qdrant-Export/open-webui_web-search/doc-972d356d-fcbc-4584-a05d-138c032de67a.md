HOLLY PITTMAN 



Fig. 17b: Drawing of side b of lapis lazuli disk with copper/bronze pin (3.4 cm 
diameter; 7 cm length with pin; confiscated from looters, Kerman 
Museum; drawing after Madjidzadeh 2003a, pg. 171 lower). 

In the Louvre museum is a lapis disk of this same type that shows a male 
and female in alternative seated and keeling postures (Amiet 1986: 296, fig. 
128). While the female wears an enveloping garment that does not reveal 
her calf and foot, the male wears a knee length skirt that shows his bare calf 
and foot.
