Rotation

Now that you’re all set up, here are what buttons you should push and in what order!
Starting out you’ll have a pet specific to your character’s race. Be sure that you always have your pet summoned/alive and attacking your target. You’ll be using Arcane Shot whenever you have enough Focus and keeping Auto Shot enabled otherwise. At level 3 you’ll get Steady Shot, which you should use whenever you don’t have enough Focus for Arcane Shot.
At level 10 you can start to transition to the rotation for your build. You’ll also be able to start taming other beast pets.
