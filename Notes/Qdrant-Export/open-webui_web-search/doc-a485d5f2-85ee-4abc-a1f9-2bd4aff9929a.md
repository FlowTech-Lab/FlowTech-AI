Hunter Pets in MoP Classic
The pet you bring to group content depends on the buff the group or raid is missing. As a  BM Hunter, you can tame  Exotic Beasts. These are special pets that can’t be tamed by  Marksmanship Hunters or  Survival Hunters.
You should have a Corehound, Waterstrider, Spirit Beast, Shale Spider, and Tallstrider available. If you play in a static guild group, where a specific buff is missing, you might want to bring a pet that covers that specific buff instead. Use our MoP Hunter Pet List to find the best pet for your situation.

Beast Mastery Hunter Stat Priority in MoP
As a  BM Hunter, you want to prioritize gear with the following stats:
Agility.
Hit and Expertise to 7.5% each.
Haste Rating.
Mastery Rating.
Critical Strike Rating.
Reforging follows this priority too.
