It seems fairly obvious that such correspondence implies a more or less 
unbroken pictorial tradition and cannot be due merely to a sudden revival 
of earlier forms at the height of the First Dynasty of Babylon. Although it is 
not yet possible to follow the paths of this tradition precisely, the figures 
which have here been singled out from the extensive repertory of the 
Hammurabi and Samsuiluna imprints can be traced back to an earlier date
