8. Season 3 Tier Set for Beast Mastery Hunter
The Tier Set is comprised of 5 items, all carrying the Midnight Herald's moniker.
In Season 3, there are two Set Bonuses per spec, one for each Hero Talent.
8.1.  Pack Leader Hero Talent Tier Set Bonus for Beast Mastery Hunter

 Hunter Pack Leader 11.2 Class Set 2pc — Increases pet damage by
5% and Beast damage by 15%. Whenever you summon a Beast, you gain 6% Haste/Crit/
Mastery for 8 seconds, depending on the Beast summoned. Boar gives Haste, Bear
gives Mastery, and Wyvern gives Crit.
 Hunter Pack Leader 11.2 Class Set 4pc — Summoning a Beast during
 Lead From the Front (aka  Bestial Wrath) causes a
 Stampede, sending out a line from your character with beasts that do
heavy damage over 7 seconds.
