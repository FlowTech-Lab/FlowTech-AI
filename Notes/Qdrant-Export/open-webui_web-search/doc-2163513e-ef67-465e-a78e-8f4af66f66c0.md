From the time of the royal cemetery at Alaca Hoyuk to the end of the 
Hittite empire, the majestic red deer |90] stag appeared in the art of Anatolia, 
nowhere with greater dignity and beauty than in the silver “stag rhyton” of 
the Schimmel collection (see figs. 8-2 and 8-3), which is considered one of 
the greatest works of ancient Near Eastern art known today. It was a vessel 
from which the king drank to the great sun goddess of Arinna and the other 
gods. 32
