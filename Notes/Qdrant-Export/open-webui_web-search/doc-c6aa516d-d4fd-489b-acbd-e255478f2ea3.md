Syria in the early Second Millennium is exemplified by the impressions 
made on Cappadocia tablets by seals carved in a Syro-Cappadocian style. 4 
Conspicuous among the figures represented on impressions and seals of 
this Syro-Cappadocian style, is a figure wearing a helmet and pictured with 
the body in front view holding a weapon in either hand. He appears on a 
cylinder in the Walters Art Gallery, PI. VIII, 1 , holding in one hand an axe
