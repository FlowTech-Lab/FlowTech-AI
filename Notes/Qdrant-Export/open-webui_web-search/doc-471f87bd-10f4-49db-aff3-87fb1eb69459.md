On the occasion of the opening of the Israel Museum in September 
1965, the Samuel Bronfman Biblical and Archaeological Museum (today 
the Samuel and Saidye Bronfman Archaeology Wing) arranged seven spe¬ 
cial exhibitions, one of which was dedicated to the Hahn-Voss Collection 
of cylinder- and stamp-seals. On this festive occasion Borowski lent several 
pieces from his own collection and prepared the catalogue of both the 
Hahn-Voss and his own objects for this inaugural exhibition.
