3. The War Within Season 3 Mythic+ Gear BiS List for Marksmanship Hunter
This list contains the Best in Slot Gear for Marksmanship Hunter from The War Within Season 3 Mythic+.
Note that this BiS List is for items from Mythic+. I am separating the BiS lists by gear sources.
This is the list you can chase when you want to improve your gear by farming Mythic+ dungeons.
The items are evaluated in a vacuum for the most part, meaning that
collecting every single item in this list and putting them together may not
actually be the highest possible DPS — but on an individual slot basis,
for the most gearsets, these items are the most likely to perform the highest.
There is no substitute for simming your own character on Raidbots if you want
to maximize your DPS.
