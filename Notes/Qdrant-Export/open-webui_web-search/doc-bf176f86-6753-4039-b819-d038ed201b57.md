8.2.  Sentinel Hero Talent Tier Set Bonus for Marksmanship Hunter

 Hunter Sentinel 11.2 Class Set 2pc —  Rapid Fire damage increased by 25%. When  Lunar Storm fades, gain the  Boon of Elune, causing your next 2  Aimed Shots to deal Arcane damage and have their damage increased by 20%.
 Hunter Sentinel 11.2 Class Set 4pc —  Sentinel's damage is increased by 25%. The  Boon of Elune is now active for the duration of  Lunar Storm at 100% effectiveness.
