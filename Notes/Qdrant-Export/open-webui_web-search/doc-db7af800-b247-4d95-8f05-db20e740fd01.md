3. Manaforge Omega Raid Gear BiS List for Beast Mastery Hunter
This list contains the Best in Slot Gear for Beast Mastery Hunter in The War Within Season 3 from the Manaforge Omega Raid.
Note that this BiS List is for items from the Raid. I am separating the BiS lists by gear sources.
This is what you send to your
Raid Leader/Loot Council when they ask you what your BiS gear is from the Raid.
The items are evaluated in a vacuum for the most part, meaning that
collecting every single item in this list and putting them together may not
actually be the highest possible DPS — but on an individual slot basis,
for the most gearsets, these items are the most likely to perform the highest.
There is no substitute for simming your own character on Raidbots if you want
to maximize your DPS.
