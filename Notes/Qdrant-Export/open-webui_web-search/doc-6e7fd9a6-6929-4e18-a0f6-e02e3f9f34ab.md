ORIENT ALIA 16, 1947 


Two styles will be discerned in the Neo-Babylonian group. They are re¬ 
ferred to here as the modelled and the cut style according to the technique 
used in the engraving of the cylinders. The modelled style shows the design 
carved on the seal in such a manner as to produce an impression of plastici¬ 
ty; the cut style comprises cylinders on which the design is composed of 
lines cut with a rotating disk which was held at different angles against the 
seal-stone. 


a) The Modelled Style
