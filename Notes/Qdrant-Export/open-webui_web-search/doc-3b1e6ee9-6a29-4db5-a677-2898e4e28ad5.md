2. Glyphs
Glyphs in Mists of Pandaria are far less important than in previous expansions, specifically because
they no longer provide damage increases.
In Mists of Pandaria Prime Glyphs have been entirely removed, Major Glyphs are mostly situational quality of life benefits in combat and
Minor Glyphs are exclusively cosmetic or tiny quality of life benefits. 
2.1. Major Glyphs
As any Hunter specialization there are two Glyphs you should pretty much always be running:
 Glyph of Animal Bond and  Glyph of Deterrence. The flat increase to healing taken
 and damage reduction upgrades are too strong to pass
up, regardless of what content you choose to engage with. There are other decent choices,
but they mostly come down to personal preference and what you value more.
