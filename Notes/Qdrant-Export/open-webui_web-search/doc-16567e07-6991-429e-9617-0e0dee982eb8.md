Really very anoying sporadic 503 errors on kojipkgs requests
Some small percentage of the time, we have been seeing 503 errors
from requests to our kojipkgs servers. This is most visible on builds,
but also started to affect composes and other things.
The setup here is somewhat complex, but a build or compose
request to kojipkgs.fedoraproject.org goes to one of two internal
proxies (proxy110/prox101), there apache terminates ssl and proxies
to a haproxy instance. That haproxy in turn has two varnish servers
behind it (kojipkgs01 and kojipkgs02). varnish on those servers
caches as much as it possibly can in memory, and anything it
cannot cache is requested from a local apache in front of a
netapp nfs server.
The problem was the haproxy -> varnish step. Sometimes haproxy
would see failed health checks and disable a backend, sometimes
that would happen with _both_ backends and clients would get
a 503.
