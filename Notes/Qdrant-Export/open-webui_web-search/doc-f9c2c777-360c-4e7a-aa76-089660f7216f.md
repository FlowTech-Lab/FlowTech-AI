Engineering —
Engineering is still by far the best profession in the game, made even stronger in Cataclysm
with the new on-use glove tinker  Synapse Springs. This new tinker is far stronger
than the Wrath version, giving Agility instead of Haste. This will be far and away the best option
as a DPS player, even in PvP. The added utility is also just fun to have in the open world and battle grounds.
 Blacksmithing —
Lets you add an extra socket to both your Gloves and Bracers thanks to  Socket Gloves and  Socket Bracer.
To be frank, as long as you have any non-gathering profession as your second profession, you are
fine. The differences in combat bonuses between all other non-engineering professions are mostly
negligible, so take your pick as to which you would prefer. Blacksmithing is generally a very strong
choice as it gives you the most control over what stats to add due to the extra sockets, but that
will always be Agility as a Hunter anyways.
