Best Talents for Leveling (All Specs)
MoP Classic uses a shared talent tree across all specs. Here’s what you want:


Level 15 – Posthaste: Extra movement speed after Disengage. Run between quests faster.


Level 30 – Binding Shot: Great AoE crowd control, especially when you overpull.


Level 45 – Spirit Bond: Passive healing that helps keep you and your pet alive during nonstop pulls.


Level 60 – Fervor: Restores Focus to you and your pet—more uptime, faster kills.


Level 75 – Blink Strikes: Massive boost to pet damage and positioning.


Simple, effective, and all geared toward making your 1-90 grind smoother.
