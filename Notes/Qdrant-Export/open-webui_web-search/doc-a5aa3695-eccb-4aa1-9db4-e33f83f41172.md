Exhilaration❌
An option that may seem good, until you notice its cooldown. Exhilaration can be great in solo fights against elites where you can’t kite and healing potions are not enough, but Spirit Bond often proves way better for longer fights in terms of healing. In addition, if you choose this talent, you will forsake the consistent 10% damage reduction from Aspect of the Iron Hawk, which is superior in both dungeons and raids.
Aspect of the Iron Hawk✅
The default option from the third tier, with many hunters prioritizing it, Aspect of the Iron Hawk grants a consistent 10% damage reduction buff. By all accounts, the damage reduction buff is stronger than Spirit Bond later on, but it also depends on your healer’s performance.
