Macros
Luckily, Survival Hunters don’t need a large amount of Macros to perform well! Like other DPS specializations, they mostly use Mouseover macros and macros that can activate multiple offensive effects at once. They may also use Cancelaura macros that might be required to turn Deterrence on and off instantly. 
Mouseover Macro
This macro can be used on any target by simply hovering your mouse over it. You can either hover your mouse over the target or the UI raid or party frame in case of friendly targets. For any beneficial spells, the macro will use “Help”, and “Harm” for any spell that can damage a target.
#showtooltip Misdirection
/cast [@mouseover, help, nodead] Misdirection
#showtooltip Explosive Shot
/cast [@mouseover, harm, no dead] Explosive Shot    
Offensive Macro
This type of macro can be used to chain together multiple offensive effects for your burst sequences! It works with multiple spells that are off-GCD and effects, such as potions or trinkets.
