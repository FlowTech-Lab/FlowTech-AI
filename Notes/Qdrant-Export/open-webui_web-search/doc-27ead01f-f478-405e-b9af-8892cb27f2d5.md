Survival Hunter AoE Rotation
In multi-target fights, a Survival  Hunter wants to follow this priority list:
Keep up  Serpent Sting.
Cast  Multi-Shot as much as possible.
Use  Explosive Trap on stacked-up targets.
Use  Black Arrow whenever it’s available.
Cast  Explosive Shot on  Lock and Load procs.
Cast  Kill Shot on enemies in execute range.
Cast  Cobra Shot.
Hunter Pets in MoP Classic
The pet you bring to group content depends on the buff the group or raid is missing. As a  Survival Hunter, you should have a Wolf, Cat, Serpent, Windserpent, and Tallstrider available. If you play in a static guild group, where a specific buff is missing, you might want to bring a pet that covers that specific buff instead. Use our interactive MoP Hunter Pet tool to find the best pet for your situation.
