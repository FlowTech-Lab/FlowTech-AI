Hence Fig. 18: IAA 1965-76 is perhaps the best candidate to be 
matched with Porada cat. 191 = Hahn 77. Shell brownish gastropod, 
36.5x22 mm. The seal is ascribed to triple group combats of slightly less 
elaborated style, therefore to be considered as slightly later in the Akkad 
period. There are two groups of battling gods, all wearing a simple horned 
crown. A god armed with a mace strikes another god, who falls back on a 
mountain, grabbing him by the miter. Porada described the mountain as 
horizontally striated, which is not the case of IAA 65-76, where the moun¬ 
tain has the usual scales. Two gods vanquish a third, held upside down by 
his legs. The god on the left raises his foot over a large vessel in a stand. 
The lack of rays seems to be in favor of the identification. Termial: sun god 
sign.
