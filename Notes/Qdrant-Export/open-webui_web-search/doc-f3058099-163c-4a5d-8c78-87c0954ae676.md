Hunter Changes in Mists of Pandaria
There were a lot of  Hunter changes from Cata to MoP. Next to the talent changes, these are the most notable changes:
 Aspect of the Hawk and  Hunter's Mark now provide their bonus in percent and not in absolute values anymore. With absolute values,  Hunters started with good damage and fell off in later phases in most expansions. The new percentage-wise bonus in MoP allows a  Hunter to scale way better in later phases.
The new glyph  Glyph of Fetch allows  Hunters to send their pets to loot the enemies. Together with the introduction of AoE loot in MoP, this improves the farming speed of  Hunter quite significantly.
Revive Pet was also reduced to a 2-second cast in patch 5.4 of the original launch of MoP. Since we’re starting MoP Classic in its 5.4 state,  Hunters will be able to revive their pets in only 2 seconds now.
