A Murder of Crows is a 2-minute cooldown that causes a target to be attacked
by a storm of crows for 30 seconds. This is numerically the best option and will do the most damage
out of any choice on this row in a single target scenario.
 Blink Strikes causes your pet to be able to teleport to targets from 30 yards
away while also dealing 30% more damage. While this is a worse option numerically,
the increase in quality of life from having a stronger, teleporting pet can be nice in some
encounters.
 Lynx Rush is a mini killing-spree style cooldown for your pet. This unfortunately
just does not do a lot of damage and is a poor choice. 

1.7. Level 90 Talents

This tier of talents will give you a new rotational damage ability.
