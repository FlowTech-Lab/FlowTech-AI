1. Marksmanship Hunter PvP Overview
While a bit weak in PvE, Marksmanship Hunters are absolutely dominant
in PvP throughout the expansion. Marksmanship has access to brutal burst windows thanks to
several powerful shots on very short cooldowns, allowing you to deal devastating damage in
just a few globals. The true strength of Hunters however comes from their unparalleled crowd
control, all of which requires no casting. Hunters have the strongest crowd control potential
in the game, with very little counter play.
2.   Strengths and Weaknesses




Strengths



Incredible burst damage potential.
Top tier crowd control.
Excellent mobility.





Weaknesses



Pet management can be challenging.
Very few defensive cooldowns.
High skill floor.
