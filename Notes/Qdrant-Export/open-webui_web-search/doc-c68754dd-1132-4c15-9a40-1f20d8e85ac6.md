Olive green serpentine - Serpentined (?) greenish calsilicate rock with 
marble fine grained, 26.2x15.7 mm. Two contesting pairs: a kilted, bearded 
hero attacks a water-buffalo. A second bearded hero in the nude attacks a 
rearing water buffalo, whose tail hangs between its legs. The arching horns

gnon.
