Gathering is def a great thing to have when leveling. Just keep in mind, herbalism can get a little tricky because they grow everywhere. Mining tends to be the easier of the two because they will always be on "rocky" surfaces and you can cling to mountain sides or ridges when traveling during questing. Skinning can be another great gathering profession, as it almost completely makes efficient time, but, probably makes the least amount of money while leveling. BTW, my level 47 warrior, just off of mining, also has 1200g, full set of glyphs etc. Servers may differ though. My server sells glyphs for no cheaper than 150g and only the little PvP glyphs can be had for under 100. The average Prime glyph that is a popular one sells for 200+
