In both cylinders a bull’s head 12 is placed upon the human head of a fig¬ 
ure, but it does not seem to be the same figure that “wears” the bull’s head. 
In the Foroughi cylinder it is the enthroned, central figure to which another 
seems to be subservient; in the Rosen cylinder it is one of the two kneeling 
male figures which raise their arms in a gesture that might signify praying 
or imploring, apparently directed toward the small figure in the center of 
the radial design. That small, central figure seems to be distinguished, like 
the central figure of the Foroughi cylinder by an enigmatic pendant band 
indicated by two lines and ending in a triple point.
