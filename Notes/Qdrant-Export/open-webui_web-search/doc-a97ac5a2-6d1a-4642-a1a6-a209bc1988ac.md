If you need more instant mobility, like a quick burst of speed, replace Crouching Tiger, Hidden Chimera with Posthaste.
Binding Shot can be swapped around depending on the kind of crowd control you need.
Regarding the damage options, Thrill of the Hunt is better in heavy AoE. The same goes for Barrage vs Glaive Toss. The first one is better in AoE and the last one in Single Target.

Best Survival Hunter Raid Glyphs
Regarding Major Glyphs, you will want to obtain Glyph of Deterrence, Glyph of Animal Bond and Glyph of Disengage. Other options are Glyph of Ice trap, Glyph of Liberation.
There are very few good Minor Glyphs, the ones worth noting are Glyph of Direction, Glyph of Fetch, Glyph of Aspect of the Cheetah, Glyph of Aspect of the Pack.
