In the summer, after her visits to the British Museum, Dr Porada would 
attend the Rencontre Assyriologique Internationale, and then spend the rest 
of the summer at Hagengut, her family’s home in Austria. Here she would 
invite friends and colleagues, and hold small Seminars and scholarly dis¬ 
cussion groups that alternated with walks in the countryside and long meals 
where delicious food and wine were served. Edith knew how to make the 
sharing of knowledge enjoyable! 

In 1984 Dr Porada retired as professor and, not surprisingly, she was 
busier than ever.
