2. Best Races for Beast Mastery Hunters
For Horde players, Orc is going to be the best option due to the additional pet damage
and  Blood Fury. Troll is a close second for damage output due to  Berserking,
the extra 1% expertise with ranged weapons, and extra damage deal versus Beasts.
However, it is also worth noting that Blood Elves have the racial  Arcane Torrent,
an AoE silence that can be incredibly strong in Challenge Modes.
As Alliance, Worgen is your best choice due to their passive 1% crit and their movement cooldown
 Darkflight. Draenei is also a strong choice because of their 1% passive hit.
