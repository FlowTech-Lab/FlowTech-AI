Very little can be said about the sealing on Plate 13C (height ca. 2.8 
cm.) which has a figure standing with its back to the inscription. The roll¬ 
ing stopped, however, before the entire figure was impressed. Therefore 
one does not know whether the figure was the only one carved on the cyl¬ 
inder or whether there was another facing the first, which seems more like¬ 
ly. For the purpose of the tablet and the record which it represented, how¬ 
ever, only the seal inscription was essential.
