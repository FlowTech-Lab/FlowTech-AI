The stats of the  Reshii Wraps are modified by the Fiber Socket, which can be swaped at any time out of
combat. These Fiber Socket Gems can be acquired at Rare/Blue quality from Hashim on K'aresh, or crafted by Tailoring at Epic/Purple
quality. As Beast Mastery, you should generally use
 Chronomantic Fiber /  Pure Chronomantic Fiber to get Haste,
although if you are already past 40% Haste, you may want to pick the Mastery or
Crit Fibers instead. It is worth simming!
