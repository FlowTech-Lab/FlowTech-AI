Haste: Decreases the time required to regenerate focus and how often you can use certain elemental powers. It speeds up your overall fights and is therefore used in multi-target battles. Also reduces the cooldown of Black Arrow, indirectly boosting the lock and Load times.


Mastery: This stat increases the damage dealt with elements. As it is mainly useful in terms of elemental damage, it is generally prioritized after all the above ones.



Optimal Talents
There are a variety of talents to choose from that provide you with several abilities, allowing you to form your attacks accordingly. However, the recommended talents for PvE are listed below:


Level 15: Posthaste - Gives a movement speed boost of 60% for 8s after using Disengage and frees from all movement impairing effects. Helps in repositioning quickly during fights.
