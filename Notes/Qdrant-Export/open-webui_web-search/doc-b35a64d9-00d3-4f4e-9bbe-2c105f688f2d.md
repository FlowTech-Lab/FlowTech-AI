5 An Early Old Assyrian cylinder was used on a tablet from Larsa, dated to the 25th year 
of Rim-Sin, reproduced in L. Delaporte, Musee National du Louvre, Catalogue des 
cylindres, cachets et pierres gravees de style oriental 2. Acquisitions (Paris 1923; here¬ 
after cited as Louvre 2) pi. 113.3 (A. 509). The animals recognizable in the lower part of 
that seal resemble those on typical Early Old Assyrian cylinders: Corpus I, 852, 854. 
Examples of Late Old Assyrian cylinders were used on tablets from Sippar (some of 
them found in excavations by Y. Scheil) published by F. Thureau-Dangin, Lettres et 
contrats de Vepoque de la premiere dynastie babylonienne (Textes cuneiforrnes, Musee 
du Louvre 2, Paris 1910) no. 74; Louvre 2, pi. 113.5a (A. 517, F, G) and no. 76, Louvre 
2, pi. 114.2a (A.520, B). None of the other seal impressions is as clearly recognizable as 
Late Old Assyrian.
