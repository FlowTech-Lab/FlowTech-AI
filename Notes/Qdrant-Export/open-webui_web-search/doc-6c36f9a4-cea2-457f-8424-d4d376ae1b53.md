Level 60: Fervor Give yourself and your pet extra Focus on a short cooldown with this ability. Dire Beast is just as good or better on a single target, but falls behind any time you are fighting more than one enemy. Thrill of the Hunt is okay if you want something passive instead so that you have one less cooldown to remember to use.
Level 75: Blink Strikes This talent will ensure that your pet is right on top of your target as soon as you are, and not lagging behind. It also consistently increases their damage. A Murder of Crows and Lynx Rush are on longer cooldowns and you will have to remember to use them in order for them to have any value.
