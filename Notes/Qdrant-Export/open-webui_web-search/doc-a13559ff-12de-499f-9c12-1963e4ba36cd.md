Posthaste gives you a 60% movement speed boost and clears all
movement impairing effects after using  Disengage. This will be the default
choice while leveling for the additional movement speed and ability to escape slows or roots.
 Narrow Escape makes your  Disengage also root nearby
enemies for 8 seconds. This is a powerful AOE control, but not as useful while leveling.
 Crouching Tiger, Hidden Chimera reduces the cooldown of your  Disengage
and  Deterrence. This is another strong option that is absolutely viable while leveling.

1.3. Level 30 Talents
This tier of talents allows you to choose a new crowd control ability.
