1.5. Level 60 Talents
This tier of talents is centered around different methods to restore focus.

 Fervor is a spell that gives you 50 focus on-use and another 50 focus
back over 10 seconds. This is the choice that will give you back the most focus overall,
but having another button to press also adds more micromanagement into your rotation.
 Dire Beast summons a pet that does some damage and passive regenerates
focus while out. This is the worst choice in the tier in terms of focus generated. However, for Beast Mastery
this has the added benefit of being an additional pet that scales with mastery, making it a far
more attractive option. This will be the default talent of choice due to the additional damage.
 Thrill of the Hunt is a passive ability that procs to cause some of your
shots to cost less focus. Numerically this is slightly worse than  Fervor assuming
Fervor is used optimally, but this choice is just a nice passive boost. It's the more convenient
option to take.
